HAIR SPECIFICATION

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Generate only the requested hairstyle.

The hairstyle must appear to belong naturally to the photographed subject.

The hairstyle must integrate into the existing photograph without changing any immutable region.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SOURCE AUTHORITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The existing scalp is authoritative.

The existing skull is authoritative.

The existing hairline is authoritative unless the requested hairstyle physically requires minimal adjustment.

The hairstyle must conform to the photographed anatomy.

Never alter anatomy to accommodate the hairstyle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIR CONSTRUCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Construct the new hairstyle using:

• existing scalp

• existing follicles

• existing growth direction

• existing skull geometry

Hair must emerge naturally from the photographed scalp.

Hair roots must appear physically plausible.

No floating strands.

No detached sections.

No artificial hairline.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIR SHAPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Modify only:

• length

• layering

• density

• texture

• silhouette

• parting

• strand flow

Only the hairstyle requested by the active Style Block may be produced.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIR COLOUR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the photographed hair colour exactly unless a colour change is explicitly requested.

Preserve:

• root colour

• mid-length colour

• ends

• highlights

• lowlights

• natural variation

Sample colour from neighbouring original hair.

Never introduce new colours.

Never beautify the colour.

Never increase saturation.

Never change colour temperature.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCALP INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Blend the hairstyle naturally into the photographed scalp.

Preserve:

• scalp visibility

• root density

• growth pattern

• natural emergence

The transition between original scalp and synthesized hair must be invisible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REALISTIC HAIR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Generate physically plausible hair.

Maintain realistic:

• strand thickness

• strand separation

• density

• layering

• flyaway hairs

• volume distribution

• edge transitions

Hair must follow gravity and natural hair physics.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do not generate:

• wig appearance

• plastic hair

• painted hair

• repeated strand patterns

• halos

• clipping

• floating strands

• broken hairlines

• unrealistic density

• artificial symmetry

• CGI appearance

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before completing the edit verify:

✓ Hair originates from the existing scalp

✓ Hair follows the photographed skull

✓ Hair colour matches the original

✓ Hair integrates naturally

✓ No visible seams

✓ No artificial blending

✓ No wig effect

✓ Only the hairstyle has changed
