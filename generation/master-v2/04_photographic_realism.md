PHOTOGRAPHIC REALISM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The completed edit must be visually indistinguishable from a real photograph.

The viewer must believe the supplied photograph was captured immediately after the requested hairstyle was professionally created.

The final result must never appear AI-generated.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PHOTOGRAPHIC CONTINUITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the photographic characteristics of the original image.

Maintain:

• camera sharpness

• lens rendering

• image noise

• camera grain

• dynamic range

• micro contrast

• edge fidelity

• optical realism

The generated hairstyle must inherit the photographic properties already present in the source photograph.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LIGHTING CONTINUITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The lighting environment is immutable.

Hair must inherit the existing:

• light direction

• light intensity

• light softness

• exposure

• colour temperature

• white balance

• shadow behaviour

• highlight behaviour

Do not introduce new lighting.

Do not modify the photographed lighting.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHADOW INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair shadows must be physically explained by the existing lighting.

Hair highlights must follow the photographed illumination.

Every generated strand must respond naturally to the existing light source.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
EDGE QUALITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair edges must appear naturally photographed.

Preserve:

• soft strand transitions

• realistic flyaway hairs

• irregular strand endings

Avoid:

• hard cut edges

• halos

• feather artifacts

• clipping

• artificial masks

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair texture must remain naturally irregular.

Generate realistic:

• strand variation

• strand thickness

• density variation

• layering

• natural imperfections

Avoid repetitive patterns.

Avoid artificial symmetry.

Avoid plastic appearance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PROHIBITED ARTIFACTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do not generate:

• CGI appearance

• over-smoothed detail

• painted texture

• waxy hair

• synthetic shine

• unrealistic reflections

• repeated strand structures

• visible seams

• floating hair

• broken transitions

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FINAL VALIDATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before returning the edited photograph verify:

✓ Hair appears naturally photographed

✓ Hair inherits existing lighting

✓ Hair inherits existing camera characteristics

✓ Hair integrates seamlessly

✓ No visible AI artifacts

✓ No visible blending artifacts

✓ No visible seams

✓ The completed image appears to be an authentic photograph rather than a generated image.
