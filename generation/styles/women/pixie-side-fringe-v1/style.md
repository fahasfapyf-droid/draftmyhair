# Pixie with Side Fringe v1 (Legacy Draft)

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Pixie with Side Fringe. The haircut is a compact Pixie whose defining feature is one deliberate diagonal fringe flowing from a side part across the forehead.

### PIXIE FOUNDATION
- Keep sides and nape distinctly shorter than the top/front.
- Maintain a compact, head-hugging silhouette with low-to-moderate crown volume.
- Keep enough top/front length to avoid a uniform crop, but no jaw-length or bob-like lower weight.
- Taper around the ears and nape without an undercut or skin fade.

### SIDE PART + FRINGE
- Establish a genuine side part at the root and direct the front section diagonally across the forehead.
- The fringe should be lightweight and eyebrow-grazing, with visible forehead/skin between natural strand groups.
- The diagonal direction must remain obvious; do not allow the fringe to fall straight down.
- Integrate the fringe continuously into the crown with no pasted-on bang block.
- Keep one side visually heavier because of the directional sweep, but do not create a dramatic asymmetrical haircut.

### CROWN / SIDES / NAPE
- Crown supports the fringe with restrained short layering.
- Sides remain close to the skull and avoid bob-like width.
- Nape is short and tapered, with no tail or neck-length extension.
- Ears may be naturally visible according to source pose; never reshape them to force visibility.

### TEXTURE / REALISM
Use fine natural strand groups, subtle directional separation and realistic short-hair density. Keep a matte-to-soft-satin photographic finish. Avoid sculpted blowout volume, spiky breakup or excessive flyaways.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Pixie with Side Fringe = compact Pixie + defined side part + clear diagonal eyebrow-grazing fringe.
Distinct from Pixie, which has no defining diagonal fringe; Parisian Gamine Pixie, which is softer and more micro-fringed; Mia Farrow Pixie, which is shorter and flatter; and Bixie, which carries more lower-side weight.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No Bixie, bob, lob, long pixie, Mia Farrow Pixie, Parisian Gamine Pixie, curtain bangs, blunt fringe, straight-down fringe, heavy triangular fringe, skin fade, hard undercut, shaved sides, bob-like nape, long tail, excessive crown volume, spiky texture, wet gel, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally cut Pixie with Side Fringe.
