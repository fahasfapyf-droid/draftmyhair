# Modern Shag

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Jaw and chin are locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Modern Shag: an all-around layered haircut with visible internal disconnection softened into continuous, wearable transitions. The defining structure is distributed layering throughout the haircut, not a single long tail.

### CROWN AND UPPER STRUCTURE
- Establish a compact-to-moderate crown with the shortest meaningful internal layers concentrated through the upper crown and upper sides.
- Allow controlled lift and natural separation, never an inflated wolf/butterfly silhouette.
- Layers begin high enough to make the shag architecture visible from front and three-quarter views.

### SIDES AND FACE FRAMING
- Create multiple short-to-medium face-framing sections integrated into the side layers.
- Framing is irregular and feathered, not a single curtain panel.
- Keep side lengths visibly layered rather than a solid bob wall.

### BACK AND PERIMETER
- Maintain a medium-to-long rear perimeter with several visible internal layer exits.
- The back remains fuller and longer than the crown without a sharply separated mullet tail.
- Ends break into natural uneven groups rather than one blunt line.

### TEXTURE
Use controlled piece separation, fine feathering and natural strand variation. Texture is visible but not chaotic. Movement is gravity-driven rather than a salon blowout.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Modern Shag = high distributed layering + feathered face framing + layered medium/long back + no single dominant tail. Distinct from Modern Mullet, Mullet, Wolf Cut, Butterfly Cut, Hush Cut and Long Layers.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No mullet, modern mullet, wolf cut, butterfly cut, hush cut, long-layer-only haircut, blunt bob, lob, jellyfish cut, disconnected long tail, shaved sides, hard undercut, excessive crown volume, theatrical feathering, uniform curtain panels, wig effect, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally cut contemporary Modern Shag.
