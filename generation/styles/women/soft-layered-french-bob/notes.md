# Soft Layered French Bob — Production Notes

STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A short, lightweight French Bob with subtle internal layering.

The defining combination is:

CHIN LENGTH
+
COMPACT PARISIAN SILHOUETTE
+
SOFT INTERNAL MOVEMENT
+
BREATHABLE DENSITY
+
DIFFUSED PERIMETER

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This engine must not become an Italian Bob.

Italian Bob emphasizes:

• density
• weight
• polish
• strong perimeter
• luxury blowout

Soft Layered French Bob emphasizes:

• lightness
• softness
• movement
• subtle layering
• natural fall

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. ITALIAN BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive density
• heavy weight line
• polished blowout
• dense perimeter

Correction:

Reduce density and perimeter weight.
Restore breathable structure.

2. BLUNT BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• hard horizontal edge
• no visible movement
• excessive precision

Correction:

Soften perimeter and introduce subtle internal layering.

3. LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• hair extends below chin
• elongated silhouette

Correction:

Shorten the perimeter to chin level.

4. BIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• insufficient lower bob weight
• overly short sides
• pixie-like silhouette

Correction:

Restore chin-level bob structure.

5. SHAG DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive texture
• disconnected layers
• choppy perimeter

Correction:

Reduce texture and restore controlled French Bob structure.

6. HELMET DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive crown fullness
• rounded artificial silhouette

Correction:

Reduce volume and make the hair follow the skull.
