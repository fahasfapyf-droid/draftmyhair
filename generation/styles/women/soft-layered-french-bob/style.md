STYLE TARGET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a genuine modern Soft Layered French Bob.

The defining characteristics are:

• short chin-level architecture
• compact Parisian silhouette
• soft internal movement
• lightweight density
• subtly diffused perimeter
• understated natural texture

The haircut must immediately read as a French Bob.

It must NOT read as:

• Italian Bob
• Structured Blunt Bob
• Blunt Bob
• Lob
• Bixie
• Pixie
• helmet bob
• heavily layered bob

The style should feel wearable, refined, effortless, and naturally
settled rather than heavily styled.

HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Determine intrinsic hair colour exclusively from the original root zone.

Read the first approximately 2–3 mm emerging from the scalp.

Use visible root areas around:

• frontal hairline
• temples
• part
• crown

Ignore environmental colour influence from:

• lighting
• clothing
• background
• reflected surroundings

Reproduce the exact intrinsic source colour throughout the haircut.

Preserve only natural tonal variation already present.

Do not:

• invent highlights
• increase saturation
• warm the colour
• cool the colour
• change gloss
• introduce artificial colour variation

LENGTH CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair must terminate at or slightly above chin level.

No strand may extend below the chin.

All original length beyond this boundary must be removed.

The final haircut must unmistakably read as a short French Bob.

Do not allow the result to drift toward:

• lob length
• jaw-length long bob
• bixie length
• shoulder length

The overall length should remain compact.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a compact, slightly rounded silhouette.

The shape follows the natural skull curvature.

The silhouette should remain close to the head.

Avoid:

• outward expansion
• triangular widening
• boxy structure
• mushroom volume
• helmet shape
• stacking
• excessive buildup

The crown remains flat and controlled.

No artificial lift.

No puffing.

The overall profile should communicate a clean Parisian French Bob.

Maintain enough weight to preserve the French Bob identity.

However, the haircut must remain lightweight and breathable.

Do not create the dense heavy weight characteristic of an Italian Bob.

Do not create a solid blunt mass.

Allow subtle internal spacing between neighbouring hair sections.

The lower section should feel controlled rather than compressed.

The haircut contains subtle internal layering.

Layering must shape movement without becoming visually obvious.

Hair follows its natural growth direction.

No crown lift.

No puffing.

No sculpted volume.

• remove excess bulk
• introduce gentle movement
• prevent a solid hair mass
• maintain lightweight structure

Layering must remain visually integrated.

Do not create:

• visible layer lines

STYLE DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ITALIAN BOB:

→ denser
→ heavier
→ stronger weight line
→ more polished blowout
→ greater visual weight

SOFT LAYERED FRENCH BOB:

→ lighter
→ more breathable
→ softer perimeter
→ subtle internal layering
→ natural movement
→ less polished
→ less dense

STRUCTURED BLUNT BOB:

→ sharper
→ cleaner
→ harder perimeter
→ minimal layering

This engine must remain distinctly softer than both.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SELF-CORRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If the result reads as Italian Bob:

→ reduce density and weight.
→ soften the perimeter.
→ remove excessive polish.

If the result reads as Structured Blunt Bob:

→ soften the perimeter.
→ introduce subtle internal movement.
→ remove hard geometric edges.

If the result reads as Lob:

→ shorten the overall length to chin level.

If the result reads as Bixie:

→ restore sufficient chin-level bob weight.

If the result reads as Shag:

→ reduce texture and disconnected layering.

If the result reads as Helmet Bob:

→ reduce crown volume and side expansion.
