# Micro Bob

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY

Modify only the hair. The face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Any natural head tilt in the source is intentional and must remain unchanged. Jaw shape and width are locked. Chin is locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. Match source scale and framing exactly; no zoom, crop or reframe.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION — CRITICAL

Preserve exact facial and visible skin texture, pores, tone and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beauty filter, retouching, relighting or skin-tone drift. Hair must not cast artificial color onto skin. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY

STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, sampling hairline, temples, part and nape where visible.

STEP 2 — Ignore and discard color from background, lighting, environment, clothing and reflections.

STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact source root color and only natural variation already present. Do not invent highlights, lowlights or dye.

STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: would the reproduced hair color still look correct on a pure white background? If no, correct the contamination.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Create a true Micro Bob: an ultra-compact bob shorter than a conventional chin-length bob, but still clearly a bob rather than a pixie or crop. The defining signal is a very short, controlled perimeter with a coherent bob weight line.

### LENGTH AND PERIMETER
- Place the dominant perimeter around the mouth-to-upper-jaw region, with the exact termination adapted to the source head proportions.
- Keep the haircut clearly above the anatomical chin or at most brushing its upper edge; it must remain materially shorter than Chin-Length Bob and Jaw-Length Bob.
- Maintain one coherent perimeter around the front, sides and visible back rather than separate cropped panels.
- Keep enough lower density to read as a bob, not a pixie or bixie.
- Do not extend the perimeter into a neck-length lob.

### INTERNAL STRUCTURE
- Use restrained internal structure with controlled lower weight.
- A subtle amount of internal graduation may support the compact shape, but the silhouette must remain primarily perimeter-driven.
- Keep the crown calm and close to the skull.
- Face framing must remain subordinate to the short bob perimeter.

### FINISH
Use realistic strand grouping, fine natural edge variation and restrained movement. The cut edge should be controlled and premium without becoming ruler-straight or CGI-perfect. Hair should fall naturally rather than behaving like a blowout.

### DIFFERENTIATION
The defining combination is ultra-short bob length + coherent compact perimeter + retained bob density. It must remain distinct from Jaw-Length Bob, Chin-Length Bob, Mini Bob, French Bob, Italian Bob, Bixie and Pixie.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No pixie, crop, bixie, jaw-length bob, chin-length bob, mini bob, lob, long bob, French bob, Italian bob, shaggy bob, heavy layering, disconnected panels, exaggerated A-line extension, high crown volume, fake wig edge, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The finished image must look like the same original photograph after a professional Micro Bob haircut, not a regenerated person or synthetic wig.
