# Mia Farrow 1968 Pixie — Production Notes

Status: LOCKED v2

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This style represents an ultra-short 1968 Vidal Sassoon-inspired pixie.

The intended character is:

• elfin
• gamine
• compact
• boyish
• feminine
• precise
• understated
• architectural

The haircut should feel historically authentic rather than contemporary.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut is defined by:

• 1–2 inch maximum length
• compact oval silhouette
• close skull relationship
• exposed ears
• tight sides
• tightly tapered nape
• mostly open forehead
• minimal crown volume

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Modern Pixie Drift

Symptoms:

• excessive crown volume
• fluffy texture
• airy sides
• modern piece-y styling

Correction:

Reduce volume and texture.

2. Bixie Drift

Symptoms:

• sides become too long
• lower perimeter becomes visible
• ears become partially covered

Correction:

Shorten sides and expose ears.

3. Crop Drift

Symptoms:

• crown becomes too short
• silhouette loses pixie character

Correction:

Restore approximately 1–2 inches of crown length.

4. Artificial Historical Styling

Symptoms:

• exaggerated vintage styling
• rigid shape
• artificial wave
• editorial finish

Correction:

Restore natural wear and quiet texture.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Texture supports the haircut.

Texture does not define the haircut.

The silhouette must communicate the style before texture is noticed.
