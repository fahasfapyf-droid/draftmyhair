# Mia Farrow Pixie

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY

Modify only the hair. The face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Jaw shape and width are locked. Chin is locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. Match source scale and framing exactly; no zoom, crop or reframe.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION — CRITICAL

Preserve exact facial and visible skin texture, pores, tone and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beauty filter, retouching, relighting or skin-tone drift. Hair must not cast artificial color onto skin. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY

STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, sampling hairline, temples, part and nape where visible.

STEP 2 — Ignore and discard color from background, lighting, environment, clothing and reflections.

STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact source root color and only natural variation already present. Do not invent highlights, lowlights or dye.

STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: would the reproduced hair color still look correct on a pure white background? If no, correct the contamination.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Create the true compact 1968 Mia Farrow-style pixie: ultra-short, quiet, precise and skull-following. The identity of the haircut comes from extreme compactness and controlled geometry, not modern texture or volume.

### LENGTH AND SILHOUETTE
- Keep the entire haircut unmistakably ultra-short; the longest crown sections should remain approximately 1–2 inches and never approach bixie, bob or jaw-length territory.
- Build a petite, compact oval silhouette that follows the skull closely.
- Keep crown elevation minimal and substantially calmer than a contemporary voluminous pixie.
- Keep the sides tight and the nape very short and cleanly tapered.
- Preserve a refined, feminine-gamine character without adding softness that turns the cut into a modern salon pixie.

### FRONT AND EARS
- Keep the forehead predominantly open with only tiny naturally settled front pieces.
- Do not create a swept fringe, curtain fringe or deliberate diagonal bang.
- Keep both ears naturally visible and contour the hair tightly around them; the ears themselves remain untouched.
- Do not create bulky side panels or long pieces around the ears.

### CROWN, SIDES AND NAPE
- Crown hair should lie close to the skull with fine, quiet strand separation.
- Sides should remain compact with no bixie-length accumulation.
- Nape should taper tightly toward the neck without a bob-like lower perimeter or disconnected undercut.
- Haircut geometry, not styling product, creates the precision.

### TEXTURE AND FINISH
Use fine, restrained strand definition with low movement and minimal volume. Allow subtle natural fibre variation, small irregularities and realistic root density. The finish should be matte-to-soft-satin and naturally settled, never wet, crunchy, spiky, tousled or editorial.

### DIFFERENTIATION
The defining combination is ultra-short length + exposed ears + tight sides/nape + low crown + open forehead + quiet 1968 precision. It must remain distinct from Boyish Pixie, modern Pixie, Bixie, French Crop, Pageboy, Mushroom/Bowl Cut and Undercut Pixie.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No modern fluffy pixie, high-volume pixie, bixie, bob, lob, jaw-length hair, side fringe, swept fringe, curtain bangs, heavy ear coverage, bulky side panels, disconnected undercut, fade, mushroom shape, helmet shape, shaggy texture, spiky texture, exaggerated crown, artificial scalp exposure, wig edge, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The finished image must look like the same original photograph after a professionally executed ultra-short 1968 pixie haircut, not a regenerated person or synthetic wig.
