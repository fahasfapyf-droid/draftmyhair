# French Bob — Production Notes

Status: v1

STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

French Bob is defined by:

1. Upper-jaw to mid-jaw length
2. Compact proportions
3. Softly rounded silhouette
4. Moderate density
5. Relaxed Parisian styling
6. Subtle natural texture
7. Restrained movement

The haircut should look professionally cut but naturally settled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

French Bob is NOT a shorter Italian Bob.

The distinction is behavioural and architectural:

French Bob:
→ compact
→ moderate weight
→ softer surface
→ relaxed styling
→ natural irregularity

Italian Bob:
→ denser
→ more luxurious
→ heavier
→ more polished
→ stronger blowout behaviour

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. ITALIAN BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive density
• excessive polish
• heavy rounded weight
• strong blowout
• overly perfect surface

Correction:

Reduce density.
Relax the surface.
Restore Parisian irregularity.

2. BLUNT BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• ruler-like perimeter
• excessive geometric precision
• hard edge
• insufficient movement

Correction:

Introduce microscopic point-cut variation.
Relax strand organization.

3. BIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• too short
• fragmented lower perimeter
• excessive ear exposure
• pixie-like proportions

Correction:

Restore jaw-level weight.
Maintain compact Bob architecture.

4. LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• length below jaw
• elongated silhouette
• neck/shoulder contact

Correction:

Restore upper-jaw to mid-jaw length.

5. MUSHROOM DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive width
• heavy crown
• rounded helmet-like silhouette

Correction:

Reduce lateral volume.
Follow the skull more closely.

6. OVER-STYLED DRIFT

Symptoms:

• salon-perfect symmetry
• rigid strand arrangement
• editorial finish

Correction:

Introduce subtle natural differences in:

• curvature
• overlap
• compression
• strand direction
