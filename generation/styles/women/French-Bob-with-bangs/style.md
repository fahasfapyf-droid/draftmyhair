# French Bob — With Bangs

Create a true modern French Bob with a classic Parisian fringe. The defining combination is compact upper-jaw-to-mid-jaw length, moderate rounded weight, relaxed natural texture, and an integrated soft French fringe.

## BOB GEOMETRY
- Keep the main bob perimeter between the upper-jaw and mid-jaw region, clearly shorter than a lob.
- Maintain a compact continuous lower silhouette with moderate weight and a softly refined perimeter.
- Keep the bob balanced and softly rounded without the denser sculpted weight of an Italian Bob.
- Use restrained internal structure; the recognizable shape must come from the compact perimeter and controlled lower weight.

## FRENCH FRINGE
- Create one clearly defined frontal fringe spanning the natural frontal hairline.
- Keep the fringe medium in density and softly point-cut rather than thick, ruler-straight, or heavily geometric.
- Let the fringe sit around the brow region while retaining small natural gaps and strand-group variation.
- Integrate both outer fringe corners smoothly into the side sections without a visible attachment seam.
- Do not turn the fringe into curtain bangs or a Korean see-through fringe.

## PARISIAN CHARACTER
- Preserve relaxed, lived-in movement rather than an editorial blowout.
- Allow subtle independent strand groups, slight asymmetry in microscopic fibre behaviour, and natural gravity-driven settling.
- Keep crown volume controlled and head-conforming.
- The overall result should feel professionally cut but naturally worn.

## STYLE DIFFERENTIATION
French Bob — With Bangs = compact jaw-level bob + moderate rounded weight + relaxed Parisian texture + unmistakable soft French fringe. The fringe is secondary to the bob silhouette but must remain visibly present.

## SURFACE AND REALISM
Maintain realistic root density, natural strand grouping, microscopic edge variation, source hair colour, original lighting response, and photographic texture.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No French Bob without bangs, curtain bangs, Korean see-through bangs, heavy blunt fringe, Italian Bob, Blunt Bob, Lob, Soft Lob, Bixie, Pixie, Shag, Wolf Cut, Mushroom Bob, exaggerated blowout, helmet volume, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, forehead, brows, eyes, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
