Create an authentic modern French Bob.

The defining characteristics are:

• compact jaw-length proportions
• softly rounded silhouette
• moderate density
• relaxed Parisian styling
• subtle natural strand grouping
• softly refined perimeter
• restrained natural movement

The hairstyle must immediately read as:

FRENCH BOB

It must NOT read as:

• Italian Bob
• Blunt Bob
• Layered Bob
• Soft Lob
• Lob
• Bixie
• Pixie
• Shag
• Wolf Cut
• Mushroom Bob

The overall character is effortless Parisian elegance rather than
highly polished luxury styling.

The haircut should look professionally cut but naturally settled.

HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Determine intrinsic hair colour exclusively from the original root zone.

Use visible areas including:

• frontal hairline
• temples
• natural part
• crown
• nape

Read the true intrinsic colour from the first approximately
2–3 mm of hair emerging from the scalp where visible.

Ignore:

• background colour
• clothing reflections
• skin reflections
• environmental colour
• colour grading
• reflected light

Preserve the original intrinsic hair colour.

Maintain only the natural tonal variation already present.

Do not:

• invent highlights
• warm the colour
• cool the colour
• increase saturation
• increase gloss
• progressively lighten the hair
• transfer reference-image colour

LENGTH CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a compact French Bob.

The finished haircut must terminate:

BETWEEN UPPER JAW AND MID-JAW

Never extend below the jawline.

Never enter Lob territory.

Maintain compact proportions.

The haircut should frame the lower face without creating substantial
neck-length or shoulder-length hair.

Do not create:

• Lob length
• collarbone length
• shoulder contact
• elongated front pieces

SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a compact, softly rounded silhouette.

The shape should gently follow the jaw and natural skull curvature.

Maintain:

• compact proportions
• controlled width
• soft rounding
• balanced weight

Avoid:

• excessive width
• exaggerated roundness
• triangular expansion
• helmet geometry
• mushroom geometry
• excessive crown volume

The silhouette should feel soft, relaxed and naturally balanced.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain moderate internal density.

The haircut should feel substantial without becoming heavy.

Allow visual weight to sit naturally around the jawline.

Avoid the dense sculpted weight characteristic of an Italian Bob.

Avoid the extremely light architecture of a Bixie.

The lower perimeter should retain enough weight to clearly establish
the French Bob silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PARISIAN CHARACTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut should communicate:

• effortless elegance
• relaxed sophistication
• natural confidence
• quiet femininity
• lived-in salon quality

It should appear professionally cut but not freshly sculpted for
an editorial photograph.

Avoid mirror-perfect symmetry.

Avoid rigid precision.

Avoid excessive polish.

The overall finish should feel as though the hair naturally settled
after a professional Paris salon blow-dry.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain compact French Bob structure while introducing subtle
natural strand grouping.

Hair should consist of multiple soft ribbon-like groups rather than
one continuous sheet.

Neighbouring groups may:

• separate slightly
• overlap
• compress
• rejoin

Allow microscopic variation in:

• fibre direction
• spacing
• overlap
• density
• curvature

Texture must emerge from natural strand behaviour.

It must never create visible layering.

Avoid:

• chunky texture
• editorial piece separation
• repeated strand patterns
• artificial curl patterns
• excessive flyaways
• plastic smoothness

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The perimeter should remain clean and professionally cut.

However, it must NOT appear mathematically perfect.

Use microscopic point-cut variation.

Individual strand groups may terminate at slightly different lengths.

Target:

98% structural precision
2% natural microscopic variation

Avoid:

• ruler-straight digital edges
• overly blunt geometry
• feathered shag-like edges
• broken perimeter

The perimeter should remain visually coherent while retaining
natural human irregularity.

Create a gentle inward curve beneath the jaw.

The inward movement should be:

• subtle
• gravity-driven
• naturally uneven
• softly settled

Avoid:

• exaggerated curling
• strong bevels
• uniform round-brush curls
• excessive inward rotation
• heavy salon volume

The ends should appear naturally settled rather than deliberately
sculpted.

Maintain subtle natural crown lift.

The crown must not appear flat.

However, volume must remain controlled.

Allow natural head-conforming volume.

Avoid:

• teased roots
• excessive lift
• crown inflation
• bubble shape
• helmet shape

Movement remains restrained.

Hair settles naturally under its own weight.

Only outer strand groups should display gentle independent motion.

Allow slight differences in:

• curvature
• overlap
• compression
• strand direction

The overall silhouette remains compact.

The haircut should never appear frozen or mechanically arranged.

OPTIONAL FRINGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Only introduce a French fringe if the requested style variant
explicitly includes one.

If fringe is requested:

Create a classic Parisian fringe with medium density.

The fringe should consist of several naturally separated strand
clusters rather than one continuous curtain.

Allow tiny irregular spacing between neighbouring groups.

The fringe should lightly skim the eyebrows while allowing portions
of the forehead to remain visible.

The edge should be softly point-cut.

Avoid:

• Korean see-through bangs
• curtain bangs
• heavy geometric bangs
• thick uniform fringe

The fringe should appear naturally worn rather than deliberately
arranged.

Introduce tiny differences in:

The styling should appear quietly imperfect.

Do not disturb the compact French Bob architecture.

The viewer should perceive authentic human styling rather than

→ denser
→ more luxurious
→ stronger rounded weight
→ more polished blowout

BLUNT BOB

→ harder perimeter
→ stronger geometric control
→ less softness

SOFT LOB

BIXIE

SELF-CORRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If the result appears too polished:

→ relax surface organization.
→ introduce subtle strand variation.
→ reduce rigid symmetry.

If the result appears too structured:

→ soften the surface.
→ increase microscopic natural variation.

If the result appears too textured:

→ restore the compact silhouette.
→ reduce visible strand separation.

If the result resembles an Italian Bob:

→ reduce density.
→ reduce polished blowout behaviour.
→ restore effortless Parisian character.

Do NOT change the fundamental French Bob length merely to correct
Italian Bob drift.

If the result resembles a Lob:

→ shorten to upper-jaw/mid-jaw proportions.

If the result resembles a Bixie:

→ restore moderate jaw-level weight.
→ reduce fragmented lower sections.
