• extends well below shoulders
• no shoulder-length termination
• no collarbone termination
• long lower mass remains intact

FAIL:

• medium length
• lob
• shoulder-length
• excessive shortening

PASS:

• layers begin around chin/collarbone
• gradual cascade
• natural irregularity
• preserved fullness
• no visible step cuts

FAIL:

• blunt single sheet
• aggressive disconnected layers
• shag structure
• wolf-cut structure

• natural taper

• no bangs
• no curtain fringe

• blurred
