STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Long Layers is a length-preserving layering transformation.

The long-hair identity must remain intact.

Core architecture:

LONG LENGTH
+
SOFT CASCADING LAYERS
+
CHIN/COLLARBONE START
+
SUBTLE FACE FRAMING
+
RETAINED FULLNESS
+
SOFT TAPERED ENDS

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do not shorten the subject into a medium-length haircut.

The transformation modifies internal structure while preserving
long-hair proportions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. MEDIUM-LENGTH DRIFT

Symptoms:

• hair terminates near shoulders
• insufficient long-hair mass

Correction:

Restore length well below shoulders.

2. BLUNT LONG HAIR DRIFT

Symptoms:

• single-sheet appearance
• little internal movement
• heavy uniform ends

Correction:

Increase soft cascading internal layering.

3. BUTTERFLY CUT DRIFT

Symptoms:

• dramatic face framing
• excessive upper layers
• strong volume contrast

Correction:

Reduce upper-layer separation and restore continuous long layers.

4. SHAG DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive texture
• aggressive breakup
• short disconnected sections

Correction:

Reduce texture and preserve long continuous structure.

5. WOLF CUT DRIFT

Symptoms:

• dramatic crown
• aggressive crown-to-length contrast
• shag-like lower structure

Correction:

Reduce crown contrast and restore softer layering.

6. OVER-THINNING

Symptoms:

• weak lower density
• excessively wispy ends
• loss of long-hair fullness

Correction:

Restore controlled density while retaining tapered ends.

TEXTURE RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Texture follows the source.

Do not impose curls or waves that are not supported by the source hair.

The haircut architecture changes.

The fundamental hair texture does not.
