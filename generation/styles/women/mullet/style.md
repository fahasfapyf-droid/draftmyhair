# Mullet

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Jaw and chin are locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a recognizable classic Mullet defined by clear short-front/shorter-side versus longer-back architecture. The rear length is deliberate and substantial while the front remains comparatively compact.

### FRONT
- Keep the forehead/front relatively short or medium-short.
- Do not turn the front into a long curtain.
- Maintain enough top length to connect naturally into the rear without a Mohawk crest.

### SIDES
- Sides are shorter than the back and clearly separated in length, but remain hair-covered.
- The side reduction creates recognizable front-to-back contrast without a skin fade or disconnected undercut.
- Keep side density natural.

### BACK
- Nape and rear are distinctly longer than sides and front.
- Maintain a continuous elongated rear section with visible weight and natural taper.
- The back forms the dominant length contrast without becoming a modern shag curtain or punk tail.

### CROWN AND TEXTURE
Keep crown volume moderate and natural. Use controlled layering and subtle texture. Avoid excessive feathering, exaggerated crown height or high-fashion fragmentation.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Mullet = clear short-front/short-side versus substantially longer-back architecture. Distinct from Modern Mullet, Modern Shag, Wolf Cut, Mohawk, Faux Hawk and Jellyfish Cut.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No modern shag, modern mullet, wolf cut, jellyfish cut, mohawk, faux hawk, long-layer haircut, butterfly cut, shaved sides, skin fade, hard undercut, disconnected top crest, excessive crown volume, extreme punk styling, exaggerated feathering, curtain-bang transformation, wig effect, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally cut classic Mullet.
