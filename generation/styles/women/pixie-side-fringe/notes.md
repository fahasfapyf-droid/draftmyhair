PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. BIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive lower-side length
• bob-like fullness
• ears becoming hidden
• hair approaching jaw

Correction:

Shorten sides and lower perimeter.

2. LONG PIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive crown length
• elongated sides
• excessive lower weight

Correction:

Reduce crown and side length.

3. STRAIGHT FRINGE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• fringe falls vertically
• no diagonal movement
• forehead covered uniformly

Correction:

Restore clear side-part origin and diagonal sweep.

4. HEAVY BANG DRIFT

Symptoms:

• dense triangular fringe
• forehead largely hidden
• no transparency

Correction:

Reduce fringe density and introduce natural air gaps.

5. PARISIAN GAMINE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• fringe becomes too short
• no defined side sweep
• micro-fringe character

Correction:

Increase fringe length slightly and restore directional sweep.

6. MIA FARROW DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessively flat crown
• overly short hair
• no directional movement

Correction:

Restore 2–3 inch crown and side-fringe architecture.
