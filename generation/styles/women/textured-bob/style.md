# Textured Bob

Create a true Textured Bob: a coherent short-to-medium bob whose primary distinction is controlled surface texture and piece separation while the underlying perimeter remains clearly bob-like.

## LENGTH / FOUNDATION
- Keep the dominant foundation around chin to upper-neck/short-bob territory, clearly shorter than a lob.
- Preserve moderate lower perimeter weight and a readable overall bob silhouette.
- Nape remains compact with no long tail or shoulder-length extension.

## STRUCTURE
- Use restrained internal weight release rather than multiple obvious short layers.
- Texture is primarily a surface and end phenomenon; do not build the haircut from visible layer shelves.
- Keep crown calm and side structure coherent.
- The perimeter remains more continuous than Shaggy Bob.

## TEXTURE SYSTEM
- Create fine-to-medium strand grouping with irregular piece separation, subtle feathering and natural dry movement.
- Texture should be visible through varied strand exits and realistic highlight/shadow grouping rather than repetitive digital pieces.
- Major sections remain connected.
- Ends can be lightly choppy but retain enough density to preserve the bob outline.

## FRONT
Use subtle integrated front movement from cheekbone through jaw only where appropriate. No dominant curtain panel, butterfly wing or wolf-style framing.

## FINISH
Touchably textured, matte-to-natural and lived-in. Preserve source texture and gravity. No beach-wave pattern, wet gloss, crunchy product, artificial flyaway explosion or repeated strand pattern.

## FAMILY POSITION
Textured Bob = coherent bob geometry + moderate perimeter weight + controlled surface texture with restrained internal layering.
Distinct from Shaggy Bob, which has stronger internal fragmentation; Layered Bob, which relies on visible structural layers; Blunt/Structured Blunt Bob, which emphasize edge precision; and Textured Crop, which is materially shorter.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No shaggy bob, shag, modern shag, wolf cut, butterfly cut, mullet, lob, long bob, pixie, bixie, blunt one-length bob, heavy graduation, disconnected layers, long face-framing wings, curtain bangs, excessive crown volume, chunky texture, beach waves, wet look, greasy hair, plastic shine, crunchy styling or wig effect.
