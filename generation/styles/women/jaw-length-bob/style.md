# Jaw-Length Bob

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY

Modify only the hair. The face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Any natural head tilt in the source is intentional and must remain unchanged. Jaw shape and width are locked. Chin is locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. Match source scale and framing exactly; no zoom, crop or reframe.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION — CRITICAL

Preserve exact facial and visible skin texture, pores, tone and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beauty filter, retouching, relighting or skin-tone drift. Hair must not cast artificial color onto skin. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY

STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, sampling hairline, temples, part and nape where visible.

STEP 2 — Ignore and discard color from background, lighting, environment, clothing and reflections.

STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact source root color and only natural variation already present. Do not invent highlights, lowlights or dye.

STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: would the reproduced hair color still look correct on a pure white background? If no, correct the contamination.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Create a true Jaw-Length Bob whose defining constraint is an actual jaw-level perimeter, shorter and more compact than a chin-to-neck bob or lob.

### LENGTH AND PERIMETER
- Place the dominant lower perimeter directly around the anatomical jawline, following the source head proportions.
- Keep the perimeter consistently compact from front through sides and into the visible back.
- The haircut must not extend into the upper neck except for tiny natural strand variation.
- Keep both sides balanced unless the source already contains meaningful asymmetry.

### INTERNAL STRUCTURE
- Use controlled internal weight with minimal layering.
- Preserve enough density through the lower half to read as a true bob rather than a pixie or heavily layered crop.
- Allow subtle nape graduation only where needed to support the compact jaw-level shape.
- Face framing remains subordinate to the jaw-length perimeter.

### FINISH
Use smooth, natural strand grouping with restrained movement and realistic ends. The geometry must remain readable without a dramatic blowout.

### DIFFERENTIATION
The defining combination is exact jaw-level length + compact bob perimeter + controlled lower weight. It must remain distinct from Chin-Length Bob, Italian Bob, French Bob, Micro Bob, Mini Bob and Lob.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No chin-length bob, micro bob, mini bob, Italian bob, French bob, blunt lob, soft lob, long bob, pixie, bixie, heavy layers, shag, wolf cut, exaggerated graduation, A-line extension, artificial wig edge, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The finished image must look like the same original photograph after a professional haircut, not a regenerated person or synthetic wig.
