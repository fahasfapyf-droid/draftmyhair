# Mohawk

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Jaw and chin are locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Mohawk with one continuous central strip of substantially longer hair from frontal top through crown toward the nape, contrasted against distinctly shorter side zones.

### CENTRAL STRIP
- The central strip is visibly narrower than the full head width and clearly dominant in length and density.
- Keep it continuous front-to-rear rather than isolated spikes.
- Allow modest natural lift while remaining physically plausible.
- Preserve realistic root direction and strand density.

### SIDE ZONES
- Both sides are materially shorter than the central strip.
- The side reduction must be unmistakable for a true Mohawk, without turning the design into a Faux Hawk.
- Keep overall side architecture balanced while retaining natural strand irregularity.

### FRONT AND NAPE
- Maintain one coherent central path from forehead region through crown into the nape.
- The nape continues the central strip rather than forming a bob perimeter.
- No conventional fringe should overpower the central crest.

### FINISH
Use controlled natural separation and realistic gravity. Moderate lift is acceptable; extreme spikes, wet gel and glossy editorial styling are not.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Mohawk = one dominant continuous central strip + materially shorter side zones + continuous front-to-nape architecture. Distinct from Faux Hawk, Mullet, Modern Mullet, Quiff, Pompadour and textured crop.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No faux hawk, quiff, pompadour, mullet, modern mullet, bowl cut, crop, pixie, generic textured top, multiple disconnected crests, artificial scalp painting, excessive spikes, wet gel, plastic hair, wig effect, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Mohawk haircut.
