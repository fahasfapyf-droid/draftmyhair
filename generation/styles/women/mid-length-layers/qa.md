LENGTH CHECK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PASS:

• final length between collarbone and upper chest
• no isolated long strands beyond boundary
• clearly shorter than long hair

FAIL:

• long-hair length remains
• hair extends substantially below upper chest
• length reads as long layered hair

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LAYER CHECK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PASS:

• visible internal layering
• layers actively shape silhouette
• natural overlapping sections
• reduced lower weight

FAIL:

• single continuous hair sheet
• barely visible layers
• disconnected step layers
• excessive choppiness

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Verify the result does NOT read as:

• bob
• lob
• shag
• wolf cut
• long layers

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRONT CHECK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Verify:

• soft face-framing layers
• beginning around jaw/chin
• taper toward collarbone
• no bangs
• no curtain fringe

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Verify original part remains unchanged.

TEXTURE CHECK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• variable density
• subtle micro-contrast
• realistic internal shadows

Reject:

• artificial smoothing
• repeated strand patterns
• excessive frizz
• editorial texture
