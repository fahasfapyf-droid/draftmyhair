# Mid-Length Layers — Production Notes

STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A genuine mid-length layered haircut.

This engine performs two simultaneous operations:

1. Removes excess original length.
2. Establishes visible internal layered architecture.

The style is not created by simply adding layers to existing long hair.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY LENGTH RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Final hair must terminate between:

COLLARBONE
and
UPPER CHEST

Any original hair extending below that region must be removed.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. LONG-HAIR DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive remaining length
• perimeter below upper chest
• long-hair silhouette

Correction:

Shorten the entire lower perimeter.

2. LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• continuous lower perimeter
• insufficient internal layering
• elongated bob appearance

Correction:

Increase visible internal layering and reduce continuous perimeter weight.

3. BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• compact silhouette
• horizontal perimeter
• insufficient length

Correction:

Restore collarbone-to-upper-chest length and layered exits.

4. SHAG DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive choppiness
• aggressive texture
• disconnected layers
• excessive crown breakup

Correction:

Restore controlled layered structure and reduce texture.

5. TRIANGULAR WEIGHT

Symptoms:

• heavy bottom
• narrow crown
• triangular silhouette

Correction:

Remove lower weight and redistribute volume through the layers.

The original part is authoritative.

The engine must not create a new part.

TEXTURE RULE

Texture must come primarily from the haircut architecture and
natural strand behaviour.

Do not manufacture texture through excessive strand separation.
