# Slick Back

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine Slick Back styling transformation using the existing haircut's actual length and structure. The defining feature is whole-hair backward direction with controlled surface organization—not a new haircut.

### DIRECTION
- Establish continuous backward flow from frontal hairline over crown toward the rear.
- Side sections also travel backward rather than forming curtain panels or forward fringe.
- Preserve natural local fibre variation while keeping global rearward direction unmistakable.

### WET / DRY CONTROL
- Default appearance is sleek and controlled but physically natural.
- Dry slick-back: visible fibres, natural separation, matte-to-natural highlights.
- Wet/product version only when appropriate: restrained localized sheen and believable clumping, never a liquid or plastic film.
- “Slick” must never mean grease, lacquer or mirror gloss.

### VOLUME / SILHOUETTE
- Reduce uncontrolled forward volume while preserving the source haircut's length and identity.
- Crown follows skull with only natural directional lift.
- Do not invent a pompadour, quiff, pixie or flattened skull.

### HAIRLINE / EARS
Preserve original hairline irregularity, baby hairs and root density. Hair follows existing ear anatomy naturally; never carve, regenerate or reshape ears.

### FIBRE REALISM
Use realistic aligned fibre groups with subtle differences in density, direction, overlap and highlight response. No broad painted surfaces, repeated strand patterns or artificial edge sharpening.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Slick Back = whole-hair backward styling + controlled surface organization + preserved haircut.
Distinct from Sleek-Back Pixie (pixie geometry), Side Part (off-center root separation), Pompadour/Quiff (frontal elevation), and Wet-Look styling (product sheen as the defining feature).

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No new haircut, pixie conversion, bob conversion, lob conversion, pompadour, quiff, exaggerated frontal lift, dramatic side part, curtain bangs, forward fringe, wet plastic film, greasy appearance, lacquered surface, artificial scalp line, painted hairline, excessive compression, flattened skull, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Slick Back hairstyle.
