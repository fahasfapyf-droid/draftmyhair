# Asymmetrical Bob

Create a true asymmetrical bob defined by a deliberate, immediately visible difference in side length.

## ASYMMETRY GEOMETRY

- One side must terminate visibly higher, around the jaw to upper-neck region.
- The opposite side must remain visibly longer, extending toward the lower jaw, upper neck, or shoulder direction according to the source framing.
- The length difference must be built into the haircut perimeter itself, not created by sweeping symmetrical hair across the face.
- The perimeter should travel continuously through the back and side sections while preserving the intentional left/right imbalance.
- The asymmetry must remain obvious from the source viewing angle without becoming exaggerated or theatrical.

## SILHOUETTE

- Maintain a controlled bob silhouette with a clean, intentional lower perimeter.
- The shorter side and longer side must read as parts of one coherent haircut, not disconnected sections.
- Do not turn the design into a generic angled lob, pixie-bob, or ordinary symmetrical bob.
- Keep face framing subordinate to the primary asymmetrical perimeter.
- Do not introduce bangs unless bangs already exist in the source hairstyle.

## TEXTURE AND WEIGHT

- Maintain controlled natural volume with enough lower weight to preserve the bob identity.
- Use realistic strand grouping and subtle independent fibre variation around the perimeter.
- The cut edge should be professionally controlled but never ruler-straight or CGI-perfect.
- Roots must emerge naturally from the existing scalp with believable density and directional flow.

## STYLE RECOGNITION

The result must immediately read as a premium asymmetrical bob. The defining visual signal is the deliberate shorter-side/longer-side relationship, not a side-swept styling effect.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No symmetrical bob, ordinary angled bob, generic lob, pixie-bob, fake side-sweep used to simulate asymmetry, or new bangs. Do not alter the face, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
