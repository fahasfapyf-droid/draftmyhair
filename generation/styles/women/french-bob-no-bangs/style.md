# French Bob — No Bangs

Create a true modern French Bob without bangs. The defining combination is compact jaw-level length, softly rounded moderate weight, relaxed Parisian texture, and a completely open forehead with no new fringe.

## LENGTH AND PERIMETER

- Keep the primary perimeter between the upper-jaw and mid-jaw region, clearly shorter than a lob.
- Maintain a compact continuous bob outline around the lower head without elongated front pieces.
- Keep the perimeter softly refined rather than mathematically blunt or heavily graduated.
- Preserve moderate lower weight so the haircut remains a bob rather than drifting into a Bixie.

## NO-BANGS REQUIREMENT

- Keep the forehead visually open.
- Do not create, lower, thicken, or invent a fringe.
- Do not convert the front into curtain bangs or short face-framing bangs.
- The front sections should connect directly into the side perimeter and remain longer than any imaginary fringe line.

## FRENCH CHARACTER

- Use moderate density and a softly rounded silhouette.
- Allow relaxed natural strand grouping and slight independent movement.
- Keep the finish lived-in and professionally cut rather than highly polished or sculpted.
- A subtle inward tendency at the jaw is acceptable, but it must remain gravity-driven and understated.

## STYLE DIFFERENTIATION

French Bob — No Bangs = compact upper-jaw/mid-jaw length + moderate weight + relaxed Parisian texture + open forehead. It must not become Italian Bob density, Blunt Bob precision, Bixie fragmentation, or Lob length.

## SURFACE AND REALISM

Use realistic root emergence, natural fibre separation, microscopic perimeter variation, and source-specific texture. Preserve the original hair colour and photographic surface behaviour.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No fringe, curtain bangs, heavy bangs, Italian Bob, Blunt Bob, Lob, Soft Lob, Bixie, Pixie, Shag, Wolf Cut, Mushroom Bob, excessive crown volume, rigid geometric perimeter, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, forehead, brows, eyes, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
