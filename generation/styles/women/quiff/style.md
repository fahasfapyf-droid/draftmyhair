# Quiff

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine Quiff defined by a shorter controlled front/sides with a moderate upward-and-backward lift concentrated at the frontal top. The front is elevated, but the overall silhouette remains lighter and less massive than a Pompadour.

### FRONT / FORELOCK
- Lift the frontal top upward and slightly backward from the forehead.
- The highest point sits near the front-to-mid top, not far behind the hairline.
- The forelock has visible separation from the forehead but remains connected to the top.
- Keep the front airy and moderate rather than creating a dense high wall.

### TOP / CROWN
- Top length is moderate and progressively settles toward the crown.
- The crown follows the skull with restrained volume.
- Maintain a clear front-lift-to-crown decline rather than a uniformly tall top.
- Hair direction is upward at the front and transitions naturally backward through the top.

### SIDES / NAPE
- Sides are shorter than the top and tapered close to the skull.
- Keep them hair-covered with no skin fade or shaved undercut.
- Nape is neat and shorter than the crown.
- Do not create bob, mullet or disconnected side architecture.

### TEXTURE / FINISH
Use natural strand grouping and light separation. The quiff should look touchable and photographically real, not lacquered or sculpted. Moderate texture is acceptable; avoid spiky tips and excessive shine.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Quiff = moderate frontal lift + lighter top mass + shorter sides + front-to-crown decline.
Distinct from Pompadour, which has a larger continuous elevated forelock/top mass; Faux Hawk, which concentrates length in a narrow central strip; and Slick Back, which lacks frontal elevation.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No pompadour, faux hawk, mohawk, slick back, flat top, high-volume blowout, vertical spike, giant forelock, skin fade, shaved sides, hard undercut, disconnected top, wet gel, plastic hair, helmet silhouette, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally cut Quiff.
