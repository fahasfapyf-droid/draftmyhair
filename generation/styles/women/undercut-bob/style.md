# Undercut Bob

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Undercut Bob: a recognizable bob-length upper layer with a deliberately shorter hidden underlayer beneath it. The defining feature is the concealed undercut architecture; the visible upper bob must remain coherent.

### VISIBLE BOB
- Establish a compact chin-to-upper-neck bob silhouette in the visible top layer.
- Maintain enough upper density to cover most of the undercut in normal frontal viewing.
- The visible perimeter should remain coherent rather than becoming a pixie or shag.

### UNDERCUT ARCHITECTURE
- Create a clearly shorter underlayer beneath the visible bob, concentrated through the lower occipital/nape region and extending laterally only as needed.
- The underlayer is materially shorter than the visible bob and reduces hidden weight.
- The transition from long upper layer to short underlayer must be physically plausible, with natural shadow/depth where the layers overlap.
- Do not create a high skin fade or exposed geometric shaved panel unless the style specifically requires visible exposure.

### UPPER / LOWER RELATIONSHIP
- Upper bob retains its own density and shape.
- Lower underlayer remains subordinate and mostly hidden.
- Around ears and nape, allow believable glimpses of the shorter underlayer only where the viewpoint naturally exposes it.

### TEXTURE / FINISH
Visible bob surface is controlled and natural with realistic fibre grouping. The undercut should read through depth and weight reduction, not through artificial dark bands or pasted-on sections.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Undercut Bob = bob-length visible upper layer + materially shorter hidden lower layer.
Distinct from Undercut Pixie by bob-length upper geometry; from Classic/Blunt Bob by the concealed shortened underlayer; and from shaved/skin-fade styles by the undercut remaining primarily hair-covered.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No pixie, bixie, lob, shag, wolf cut, mullet, bowl cut, skin fade, high fade, exposed shaved panel, disconnected mohawk channel, fully shaved nape, continuous one-length bob, heavy visible underlayer, wig effect, artificial dark band, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Undercut Bob.
