# Pompadour

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine Pompadour defined by a prominent elevated forelock and continuous swept-back top mass, with shorter controlled sides and a clearly structured front-to-back rise.

### FORELOCK / FRONT
- The front hairline transitions into the longest and most elevated section.
- Build a substantial forelock that rises upward and then sweeps back rather than falling forward.
- The front peak is the visual center of the haircut, with the greatest height and directional movement concentrated near the frontal top.
- Keep the front continuous with the crown; no separate quiff-like panel.

### TOP / CROWN
- Maintain a long top zone that carries the raised volume from the forelock into the crown.
- Height should be controlled and believable, with a smooth continuous arc rather than a vertical spike.
- Crown volume gradually decreases toward the rear while retaining enough mass to support the pompadour silhouette.
- Root direction must visibly support upward-and-backward growth.

### SIDES / NAPE
- Sides are materially shorter than the top and cleanly tapered, but remain hair-covered.
- No skin fade, shaved channel or disconnected undercut.
- Nape is shorter and controlled, allowing the elevated top to remain dominant.
- Side width stays close to the skull so the silhouette is top-heavy rather than helmet-wide.

### SURFACE / FINISH
Use natural strand grouping and controlled separation. The surface may be polished but must retain photographic fibre variation and realistic gravity. Avoid wet gel, plastic shine, rigid shell texture or perfectly mirrored strands.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Pompadour = prominent elevated forelock + continuous high top mass + backward sweep + shorter sides.
Distinct from Quiff, which has a lighter front lift and less sustained top mass; Faux Hawk, which uses a narrow central strip; Mohawk, which has materially shorter side zones; and Slick Back, which is flatter and lacks the defining elevated forelock.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No quiff, faux hawk, mohawk, slick back, flat top, crop, pompadour fade, skin fade, shaved sides, hard undercut, disconnected top, vertical spike, giant helmet volume, wet gel, plastic hair, artificial shine, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Pompadour.
