# Modern Mullet

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Jaw and chin are locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a contemporary Modern Mullet defined by a deliberate short-front/shorter-side/longer-back progression with a controlled fashion-forward silhouette. It must read as modern rather than 1980s or punk.

### FRONT AND CROWN
- Keep the front comparatively short and controlled.
- Maintain a compact crown with moderate texture and restrained lift.
- Transition continuously into the rear; no disconnected shelf.

### SIDES
- Keep sides visibly shorter than the back, but hair-covered and naturally integrated.
- No shaved channels, skin fades or undercuts.
- Side length sits between the short front/crown and longer rear.

### BACK / NECK
- The defining feature is a clearly longer rear section through the nape.
- Back length is substantial enough to distinguish the style from a shag, but not an extreme classic rock tail.
- Keep a narrow, elongated rear silhouette with controlled density.
- Crown-to-back transition is visibly layered and continuous.

### TEXTURE AND MODERNITY
Use separated but controlled layers, moderate piece definition and natural gravity. Keep the silhouette slightly edgy but believable. Avoid excessive feathering, huge crown volume or exaggerated tail length.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Modern Mullet = short controlled front + shorter hair-covered sides + layered elongated back + moderate contemporary texture. Distinct from Mullet, Modern Shag, Wolf Cut, Shag, Mohawk, Faux Hawk and Jellyfish Cut.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No classic 1980s mullet, extreme punk mullet, shag, wolf cut, modern shag, mohawk, faux hawk, jellyfish cut, skin fade, shaved sides, hard undercut, disconnected shelf, giant crown, exaggerated tail, heavy feathering, wig effect, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally cut contemporary Modern Mullet.
