# Structured Blunt Bob

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a premium Structured Blunt Bob defined by precision jaw-length geometry, continuous blunt density and minimal internal layering. The perimeter—not texture or styling—is the primary identity.

### LENGTH / SILHOUETTE
- Establish a precise jaw-length perimeter, with the dominant cut line around the anatomical jaw region.
- Keep the silhouette compact, balanced and visually straight while following the skull naturally.
- No section extends noticeably below the jaw or finishes substantially above the chin.

### WEIGHT / INTERNAL STRUCTURE
- Maintain near-uniform density from crown through perimeter.
- Internal layering is minimal to non-existent and must remain visually invisible.
- Do not hollow the interior or create graduation, stacking or fragmented sections.
- Crown stays close to the skull with no unnecessary lift.

### PERIMETER
- Create a continuous, sharply defined blunt edge.
- Preserve microscopic natural fibre variation so the line looks professionally cut rather than digitally ruler-drawn.
- Ends may make a subtle inward bend from natural haircut weight.
- No feathering, tapering, shredding or diffused edge.

### SURFACE / TEXTURE
Surface is smooth and refined, but individual fibres remain physically present at close inspection. Movement is restrained and gravity-driven. Precision, density and stability define the style.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Structured Blunt Bob = jaw-length + continuous blunt perimeter + uniform density + minimal layering.
Distinct from Classic Bob by stronger geometric precision; Italian Bob by less rounded/voluminous styling; French Bob by denser, sharper structure; Soft Layered Bob by absence of visible internal movement; Blunt Lob by shorter jaw-level geometry.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No Italian bob, French bob, classic rounded bob, soft layered bob, layered bob, shaggy bob, textured bob, blunt lob, lob, micro bob, pixie, bixie, visible layers, graduation, stacking, feathering, wispy perimeter, excessive movement, blowout volume, mushroom shape, helmet shape, plastic hair, wet look, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Structured Blunt Bob.
