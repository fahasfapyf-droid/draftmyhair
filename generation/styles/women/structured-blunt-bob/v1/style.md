# Structured Blunt Bob

Style Name: Structured Blunt Bob

Category: Women's Haircut

Length: Short

Difficulty: High

Primary Characteristic:

A precision jaw-length blunt bob defined by a clean geometric silhouette, uniform internal density, minimal layering, and a sharply refined perimeter that follows the natural head shape with controlled structure and understated elegance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create an authentic Structured Blunt Bob through precision haircut architecture rather than styling.

The finished hairstyle must immediately read as a professionally executed blunt bob with exceptional structural control, premium salon precision, and minimal visual texture.

The haircut should never resemble an Italian Bob, French Bob, Soft Lob, or layered bob.

Create a clean, balanced, jaw-length silhouette with strong geometric definition.

The haircut should follow the natural contour of the jaw while remaining visually straight, compact, and symmetrical.

The crown should closely follow the natural skull without visible lift, expansion, or unnecessary fullness.

The hairstyle should appear controlled from every viewing angle.

Maintain a classic jaw-length perimeter.

The hair should terminate precisely around the jawline while remaining consistent across the entire outline.

No section should extend noticeably below the jaw or finish significantly above the chin.

Distribute visual weight evenly throughout the haircut.

The hairstyle should maintain a consistent internal density from crown to perimeter while avoiding unnecessary heaviness or visible thinning.

Weight should remain balanced across both sides of the haircut.

Visible layering should be minimal to non-existent.

The haircut should rely on perimeter precision rather than internal texture to create its visual identity.

Any internal refinement should remain invisible while supporting natural movement.

The perimeter should remain the defining feature of the haircut.
