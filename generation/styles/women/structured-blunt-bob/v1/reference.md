━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Structured Blunt Bob is a precision haircut emphasizing clean geometry, uniform density, and a sharply defined jaw-length perimeter.

Unlike softer bob variations, it minimizes layering and movement to create a polished architectural appearance.
