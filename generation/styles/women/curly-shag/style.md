# CURLY SHAG

Create a genuine Curly Shag. The defining architecture is medium-to-long distributed shag architecture, shorter crown and upper-side layers, integrated face framing, preserved rear foundation, natural curl shrinkage and varied clumps.

Preserve the exact source face, expression, skin texture and tone, head position, skull geometry, jaw, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, framing, perspective, focus, grain and color balance. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate, reshape, enlarge, reduce, reposition, or reconstruct the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes.

The result must look like a real professional transformation photographed in the original scene. Hair/facial hair must emerge naturally from the scalp/skin with realistic density, roots, shadow, strand variation and edge integration. Do not introduce a wig effect, pasted edge, halo, artificial smoothing, plastic texture or digital mask boundary.

STYLE DISTINCTION:
Curly Shag must be immediately distinguishable from Wolf Cut, Mullet, straight shag, Curly Bob, uniform spiral curls.

NEGATIVE:
No Wolf Cut, Mullet, straight shag, Curly Bob, uniform spiral curls; no facial changes; no skin changes; no ear regeneration; no skull reshaping; no head-angle change; no lighting change; no background change; no crop/zoom/reframe; no artificial hairline; no synthetic texture.


## SKIN PRESERVATION — ZERO TOLERANCE

Do not lighten, darken, smooth, blur, repaint or reconstruct facial or exposed scalp skin. Preserve source pores, texture, tone, highlights and shadows exactly. Do not introduce a generic pale, grey or relit skin surface.

## ROOT AUTHORITY

Read true source hair color and root architecture only from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, hairline, temples, part and nape. Ignore background, lighting, clothing, reflections and global grading. Reproduce source root color, density, growth direction and natural variation. Do not invent root patterns.

## PHOTOREALISTIC INTEGRATION

Hair must emerge naturally from the scalp with realistic density, roots, strand hierarchy, growth direction, contact shadows, flyaways and edge behavior. Preserve original lighting, exposure, camera grain, noise, focus and color response. No wig effect, hard mask, halo, painted scalp, plastic texture or synthetic strand repetition.
