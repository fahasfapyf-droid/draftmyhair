# Pixie

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hair-adjacent area required to reveal anatomy naturally hidden by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, white balance, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. The hairstyle must conform to the source skull rather than changing head geometry. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, airbrushing, relighting, brightening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone. Hair must not cast artificial color onto the face or exposed neck.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true intrinsic hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact root color with only natural variation already present in the source.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine classic-modern Pixie: a compact short haircut with a clearly shorter perimeter and sides/nape, a controlled longer top/front, and a head-hugging silhouette. This is the base Pixie architecture, not a specialized fringe, curl, undercut, historical or bixie variant.

### CORE PROPORTIONS
- Keep the sides and nape distinctly shorter than the top and front.
- Maintain approximately 1.5–3 inches of visual top/front length, adapting to source density and perspective without changing head geometry.
- The top/front must have enough length to read as a pixie rather than a uniform crop.
- Keep the overall silhouette compact and close to the skull with restrained crown projection.
- Do not allow the lower perimeter to accumulate into jaw-length or bob-like mass.

### FRONT / FOREHEAD
- Keep the forehead predominantly open or naturally covered only by a short, integrated front section.
- Do not impose a defining side fringe, curtain fringe or heavy blunt fringe.
- Front length should flow naturally from the top rather than forming a separate bang block.

### SIDES / EARS
- Taper the sides cleanly toward the ears while preserving realistic density and natural hairline irregularity.
- The ears may be naturally visible where the haircut length permits, but their shape, position and size remain completely locked.
- Do not create an undercut or shaved plane; the sides are short through professional tapering, not skin exposure.

### NAPE
- Keep the nape short, neat and closely tapered.
- The lower rear must remain clearly shorter than the crown and must not form a bob perimeter.
- Follow the source neck geometry exactly without reshaping or exposing anatomy unnaturally.

### CROWN / TEXTURE
Use restrained natural texture and small coherent strand groups. Maintain enough separation to show individual fibres without creating spiky, shaggy or editorial texture. Crown volume is low-to-moderate and follows the skull. Ends show microscopic natural irregularity.

### PARTING / DIRECTION
Do not invent a strong side part, middle part or directional sweep. Preserve the source growth direction unless the pixie architecture requires only a subtle natural redirection of the existing top/front hair.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Pixie = compact short silhouette + shorter tapered sides/nape + controlled longer top/front + low-to-moderate crown.
Distinct from Boyish Pixie by allowing slightly softer top/front length and a more neutral salon silhouette; from Bixie by remaining clearly above jaw length; from Mia Farrow Pixie by avoiding its ultra-short historical precision; from Undercut Pixie by using no clipped undercut; and from Pixie with Side Fringe by using no defining diagonal fringe.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No Bixie, bob, lob, jaw-length perimeter, long pixie, boyish crop, Mia Farrow historical pixie, undercut pixie, disconnected pixie, side-fringe pixie, curtain bangs, heavy blunt fringe, bowl cut, mushroom cut, shaggy pixie, wolf cut, mullet, mohawk, skin fade, shaved sides, hard undercut, exaggerated crown, high volume, spiky texture, wet gel, plastic hair, artificial scalp exposure, wig effect, pasted edges, facial changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed true Pixie, with compact proportions and unmistakable short-hair architecture.
