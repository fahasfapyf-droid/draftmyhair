STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A short curly pixie defined by a compact silhouette and natural 2C-to-loose-3A curl architecture.

The haircut must read as a pixie before the viewer notices the curl pattern.

The lower perimeter must remain light.

PRIMARY STYLE RISKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. CURLY BOB DRIFT

Lower curls become too long or accumulate around the jaw.

Correction:

Shorten lower sections and increase crown-to-nape taper.

2. BIXIE DRIFT

Mid-length sections retain too much weight.

Correction:

Shorten overall length and tighten the lower sides.

3. CURLY CROP DRIFT

Hair becomes excessively compressed and loses soft crown curl volume.

Correction:

Restore controlled crown curl mass.

4. CURLY SHAG DRIFT

Layering and face framing become excessive.

Correction:

Remove long face-framing pieces and tighten the silhouette.

5. ROUNDED CURLY CUT DRIFT

Side volume becomes too symmetrical or mushroom-like.

Correction:

Reduce side mass and restore stronger crown-to-nape taper.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The curls must look naturally formed by the haircut.

Do not make every curl equally defined.

Natural variation is mandatory.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL COMPRESSION RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Foreground curls should overlap.

Some curls must partially obscure others.

Avoid displaying a collection of isolated perfect ringlets.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
