# Soft Curly Pixie

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Soft Curly Pixie. Pixie geometry is primary; a natural 2C–3A curl pattern is the texture system. The result must remain compact, short and tapered rather than becoming a curly bob, bixie or curly crop.

### PIXIE GEOMETRY
- Visible curl length is approximately 2–3 inches after natural shrinkage.
- Crown/top carries the greatest controlled mass.
- Sides and nape are distinctly shorter, softly tapered and close to the skull.
- Nothing extends below the jaw or creates a bob-like lower perimeter.
- Ears remain naturally visible where the haircut exposes them.

### CURL PATTERN — 2C–3A
- Use loose 2C waves through open areas transitioning into soft 3A curls where natural.
- Curl diameters must vary; mix open bends, partial spirals and softer compressed curl groups.
- Do not make every curl equally closed, equally sized or equally defined.
- Curl groups overlap and partially occlude one another; some merge into neighbouring groups.
- Natural shrinkage is present, but never so strong that the hair becomes an oversized rounded mass.

### FRINGE / FRONT
- Short, soft irregular curly fringe may partially reveal the forehead.
- Use small natural curl groups rather than decorative ringlets.
- No dominant center curl, curtain fringe or long curly bang.

### TEXTURE / FINISH
Hair looks naturally dried and touchable: moderate curl definition, fine fibre separation, subtle localized frizz and natural strand irregularity. No wet/crunchy/product-heavy finish, plastic curls or artificial curl symmetry.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Soft Curly Pixie = compact tapered pixie geometry + 2C–3A curl architecture + soft low-volume finish.
Distinct from Curly Bob/Bixie by shorter sides and nape; Curly Crop by softer crown mass and less severe compression; Curly Shag by absence of long distributed layers.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No curly bob, bixie, curly crop, curly shag, rounded curly cut, wolf cut, long curls, curls below jaw, curls touching neck/shoulders, oversized crown, wide sides, heavy lower perimeter, decorative ringlets, identical curls, curtain bangs, wet look, crunchy styling, plastic curls, excessive gloss, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Soft Curly Pixie.
