# SOFT CURLY PIXIE
# Draft My Hair Style Engine — v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE TARGET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a genuine modern Soft Curly Pixie.

The hairstyle must immediately read as:

SHORT CURLY PIXIE

The curl pattern supports the haircut architecture.

The curl pattern must never overpower the pixie silhouette.

The hairstyle should feel:

• soft
• light
• feminine
• compact
• natural
• effortless
• naturally lived-in

The finished haircut must remain clearly distinct from:

• Curly Bob
• Bixie
• Curly Crop
• Curly Shag
• Rounded Curly Cut

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Visible curl length should be approximately 2–3 inches after natural curl shrinkage.

Sides remain short.

Nape remains cropped and softly tapered.

Nothing should extend below the jaw.

No curls should touch:

• neck
• shoulders
• clothing

The proportions must remain pixie-length regardless of the source hair's original length.

Do not preserve excess source length simply because it exists in the original photograph.

The haircut architecture takes priority over the original length.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SILHOUETTE — PRIMARY DEFINING FEATURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a compact pixie silhouette.

Hair follows the skull naturally.

The greatest visual mass is concentrated across the upper crown.

Visual mass gradually decreases toward:

• temples
• ears
• nape

The sides must remain noticeably lighter than the crown.

The lower perimeter progressively releases weight toward the nape.

The silhouette must never become:

• round
• wide
• mushroom-shaped
• helmet-like

The viewer should recognize a pixie before noticing the curls.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Concentrate visual weight through:

• upper crown
• upper temples

Gradually reduce weight through:

• lower temples
• ear region
• nape

The lower perimeter must remain light.

Do not create a heavy lower outline.

Do not create a bob perimeter.

Do not allow curl accumulation beneath the jaw.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a natural curl pattern visually between 2C and loose 3A.

The curls are part of the haircut itself.

They must not appear added as a separate styling effect.

Use:

• soft irregular curls
• mixed curl diameters
• varied curl openness
• varied curl orientation
• partially open curls
• occasional loose spirals

Do not create repeated curl geometry.

Do not create identical curl spacing.

Do not create decorative ringlets.

Do not make every curl equally defined.

The curl structure must remain organic.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL DIAMETER VARIATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Neighbouring curls should vary subtly in:

• diameter
• closure
• length
• orientation
• density
• visual prominence

Some curls may form loose spirals.

Others may remain partially open.

Others may compress into softer wave-curl transitions.

Avoid a repetitive curl pattern.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL COMPRESSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Natural curls compress against neighbouring curls.

Allow localized flattening where curl groups meet.

Some curls should partially obscure others.

Not every curl should remain independently visible.

Create overlapping curl groups.

Depth should emerge through natural occlusion and compression.

Avoid displaying isolated, perfectly separated curls.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL IMPERFECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Avoid a collection of uniformly perfect curls.

Introduce subtle natural inconsistency.

Allow:

• partially relaxed curls
• slight curl collapse
• occasional stretched curl
• minor overlap
• tiny localized frizz
• subtle variation in curl closure

No two foreground curls should have identical:

• closure
• spacing
• orientation
• prominence

The result should resemble naturally dried curls after ordinary daily wear.

Do not create a freshly styled salon-curl appearance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRINGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a short, soft curly fringe.

The forehead should remain partially visible.

Use small irregular curl clusters.

Do not create:

• dominant centre curl
• matching forehead ringlets
• heavy fringe
• curtain bangs
• long curly fringe
• decorative spiral curls

The fringe supports the pixie architecture.

It must never become the primary visual feature.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SIDES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Keep the sides close to the head.

Create a natural taper around the ears.

Both ears should remain partially visible.

Do not create wide side volume.

Do not create puffiness.

Do not allow curls to accumulate heavily around the lower ears.

The side silhouette must remain clearly pixie-like.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
NAPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a short, softly tapered nape.

The nape must remain clearly cropped.

Do not allow curl accumulation at the neckline.

Do not create:

• grown-out nape
• long curly back
• bob-like back coverage
• heavy neckline volume

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crown carries the greatest visual mass.

Maintain soft curl volume without creating excessive expansion.

The crown should remain compact enough to preserve the pixie silhouette.

Allow natural curl compression and overlapping curl groups.

Avoid:

• oversized crown
• mushroom crown
• helmet shape
• inflated volume

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create natural strand grouping beneath the curl structure.

Maintain:

• fine strand separation
• soft matte finish
• subtle flyaways
• natural fibre irregularity

Avoid:

• wet appearance
• crunchy styling
• product-heavy appearance
• plastic curls
• excessive gloss
• AI-smooth surfaces
• artificial curl definition

The hair should look naturally dried.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CURL DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Curl definition should remain moderate.

Do not sharpen every curl.

Some curl boundaries should merge naturally.

Some curl groups should partially disappear into neighbouring groups.

The result must retain realistic photographic complexity.

Avoid artificial "perfect curl" rendering.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair follows natural growth direction.

Curl orientation should vary naturally.

Avoid mirrored curl placement.

Avoid repeated spiral direction.

Avoid identical curl trajectories.

Gravity should influence the curl groups.

Movement should remain soft and natural.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ROOT INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair must emerge naturally from the existing scalp.

Maintain realistic root density.

The transition from scalp to curl formation must be gradual.

Avoid:

• painted-on curls
• artificial scalp boundary
• uniform root spacing
• floating curls
• wig effect

Shorter hair near the roots should remain visibly connected to the longer curl structure.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VOLUME CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Volume follows the skull.

The hairstyle may have natural curl expansion, but it must not expand the apparent head disproportionately.

Maintain a compact overall silhouette.

No artificial lift.

No excessive side volume.

No oversized crown.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REALISM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair must appear naturally grown from the scalp.

Maintain:

• realistic curl density
• natural gravity
• authentic fibre behaviour
• natural curl overlap
• believable root integration
• source lighting response
• source grain
• source sharpness

The curls must respond to the original photograph's lighting.

Do not introduce a generic studio lighting appearance.

The finished image should look like the same photograph of the same person after receiving a professional Soft Curly Pixie haircut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CURLED PIXIE:

Short silhouette first.

Curl texture second.

If the result is being perceived primarily as a curly hairstyle rather than a pixie:

→ reduce lower length
→ tighten side volume
→ strengthen crown-to-nape taper
→ restore compact pixie architecture

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SELF-CORRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If the result reads as a Curly Bob:

→ reduce lower perimeter weight
→ increase crown-to-nape taper
→ shorten the back

If the result reads as a Bixie:

→ shorten overall length
→ reduce mid-length weight
→ tighten the lower sides

If the result reads as a Curly Crop:

→ soften the silhouette
→ restore natural curl volume at the crown
→ reduce overly compressed sides

If the result reads as a Curly Shag:

→ tighten the silhouette
→ remove long face-framing pieces
→ reduce visible layering

If the result reads as a Rounded Curly Cut:

→ break symmetry
→ reduce side mass
→ restore stronger crown-to-nape taper
