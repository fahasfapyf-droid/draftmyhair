# Soft Curly Pixie

Create a true Soft Curly Pixie. Pixie geometry is primary; natural 2C–3A curl behavior is the texture system. The result must remain compact, tapered and short rather than becoming a curly bob, bixie or curly crop.

## PIXIE GEOMETRY
- Visible curl length is approximately 2–3 inches after natural shrinkage.
- Crown/top carries the greatest controlled mass.
- Sides and nape are distinctly shorter, softly tapered and close to the skull.
- Nothing extends below the jaw or forms a lower bob perimeter.
- Keep the overall silhouette compact and skull-following.

## CURL ARCHITECTURE — 2C–3A
- Use loose 2C waves through some areas transitioning naturally into soft 3A curls.
- Curl diameter varies across the head; combine open bends, partial spirals and compressed curl groups.
- Do not repeat identical curl size, closure, direction or spacing.
- Curl groups overlap, merge and partially occlude each other like real hair.
- Natural shrinkage is visible but controlled; do not inflate the head into a rounded curly mass.

## FRONT / FRINGE
A short irregular curly front may partially reveal the forehead. Use small natural curl groups, not decorative ringlets. No dominant center curl, curtain fringe or long curly bang.

## SIDES / NAPE
Tapered sides and nape remain visibly shorter than the crown. Preserve realistic curl compression and density changes as hair approaches the shorter areas. Do not create a fade unless the source style specifically calls for one.

## TEXTURE / FINISH
Naturally dried, touchable hair with moderate curl definition, fine fibre separation, subtle localized frizz and authentic irregularity. No wet/crunchy product coating, plastic curls or artificial symmetry.

## STYLE DIFFERENTIATION
Soft Curly Pixie = compact tapered pixie + 2C–3A curl architecture + soft low-volume finish.
Distinct from Bixie/Curly Bob through shorter sides and nape; from Curly Crop through softer crown mass and less severe compression; from Curly Shag through absence of long distributed layers.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No curly bob, bixie, curly crop, curly shag, rounded curly cut, wolf cut, long curls, curls below jaw, curls touching neck/shoulders, oversized crown, wide sides, heavy lower perimeter, decorative ringlets, identical curls, curtain bangs, wet look, crunchy styling, plastic curls, excessive gloss or wig effect.
