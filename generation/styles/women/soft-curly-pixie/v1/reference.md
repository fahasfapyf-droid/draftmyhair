Modern Soft Curly Pixie.

Key characteristics:

• compact pixie silhouette
• upper-crown weight
• tapered sides
• cropped tapered nape
• short curly fringe
• soft 2C-to-loose-3A curl structure
• mixed curl diameter
• natural curl compression
• organic curl irregularity
