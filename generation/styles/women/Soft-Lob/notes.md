# Soft Lob — Production Notes

Status: v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Soft Lob is defined by:

1. True Lob length
2. Soft continuous perimeter
3. Invisible internal layering
4. Gentle face framing
5. Medium balanced density
6. Natural movement

The haircut should feel wearable, refined and naturally settled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Soft Lob is NOT simply a less blunt Blunt Lob.

Its identity comes from the combination of:

• softened perimeter
• invisible internal layering
• gentle face framing
• controlled natural movement

The Lob silhouette must remain dominant.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. LONG BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• insufficient movement
• overly blunt perimeter
• little internal softness

Correction:

Soften the perimeter.
Introduce subtle invisible internal layering.

2. BLUNT LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• perimeter too architectural
• lower edge too dense
• movement too restrained

Correction:

Increase point-cut variation.
Release small amounts of lower-edge weight.

3. BUTTERFLY DRIFT

Symptoms:

• visible cascading layers
• dramatic face framing
• excessive vertical movement

Correction:

Reduce layer hierarchy.
Reconnect the haircut into one Lob silhouette.

4. LONG-LAYER DRIFT

Symptoms:

• clearly visible internal layers
• excessive length
• disconnected sections

Correction:

Restore true Lob proportions.
Make internal layers invisible.

5. ITALIAN BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• too short
• excessive density
• rounded blowout
• compact silhouette

Correction:

Restore Lob length.
Reduce density.
Remove blowout volume.

6. TOO-AIRY DRIFT

Symptoms:

• weak perimeter
• transparent ends
• excessive separation

Correction:

Restore medium density.
Strengthen the Lob perimeter.

7. TOO-PERFECT DRIFT

Symptoms:

• artificial continuous surface
• ruler-like perimeter
• synchronized strands

Correction:

Introduce microscopic natural fibre variation without weakening
the overall silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The perimeter must remain clearly readable.

Unlike a Blunt Lob, it should not appear mathematically rigid.

Target:

98% structural clarity
2% microscopic natural variation

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MOVEMENT RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Movement is visible but subordinate to the silhouette.

The hair should appear naturally worn after several hours of
ordinary movement rather than freshly arranged.
