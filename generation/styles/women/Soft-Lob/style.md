Create an authentic modern Soft Lob.

The defining characteristics are:

• true Lob length
• soft elongated silhouette
• gentle natural movement
• invisible internal layering
• refined face framing
• softened perimeter
• balanced medium density

The hairstyle should communicate effortless refinement through
softness, balance and natural movement rather than dramatic layering
or sharp architectural structure.

The finished hairstyle must immediately read as a true Soft Lob.

It must NOT read as:

• Long Bob
• Blunt Lob
• Italian Bob
• French Bob
• Butterfly Cut
• Long Layers
• Shag
• Bixie
• Pixie
• Generic layered haircut

Determine intrinsic hair colour exclusively from the original root zone.

Use visible areas including:

• frontal hairline
• temples
• natural part
• crown
• nape

Read the true intrinsic colour from the first approximately
2–3 mm of hair emerging from the scalp where visible.

Ignore:

• background colour
• clothing reflections
• skin reflections
• environmental colour
• colour grading
• reflected light

Preserve the original intrinsic hair colour.

Maintain only the natural tonal variation already present.

Do not:

• invent highlights
• warm the colour
• cool the colour
• increase saturation
• increase gloss
• progressively lighten the hair
• transfer reference-image colour

LENGTH CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create true Lob length.

The finished haircut must terminate between:

COLLARBONE
and
TOP OF SHOULDERS

Maintain true Lob proportions.

Do not shorten into Bob territory.

Do not extend into long layered hair.

The front, sides and back should maintain a coherent Lob-length
relationship.

The perimeter remains visually continuous across the front.

Allow only natural variation caused by:

• gravity
• perspective
• individual fibre behaviour

SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a softly elongated silhouette.

The outline should gently follow:

• neck
• shoulders
• natural head geometry

Maintain a relaxed, balanced and naturally feminine shape.

Avoid:

• excessive roundness
• triangular widening
• dramatic graduation
• excessive lower weight
• strong A-line geometry
• excessive inward curvature

The silhouette should feel soft rather than architectural.

WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain medium visual density throughout the haircut.

Remove enough internal weight to create softness while preserving
healthy fullness.

The haircut should feel:

• balanced
• lightweight
• healthy
• naturally full

Avoid the dense sculpted weight characteristic of an Italian Bob.

Avoid the extremely lightweight architecture of a Butterfly Cut.

The lower third should retain sufficient density to preserve the
Lob silhouette.

The layers exist to soften the haircut and create natural movement.

They must NOT become the primary visual feature.

• seamless
• gradual
• invisible
• naturally integrated

Do not create:

• obvious cascading layers
• visible step layers
• disconnected sections
• dramatic layer hierarchy
• shag structure

The haircut must continue to read primarily as a Lob.

CHIN
and
COLLARBONE

The framing must blend seamlessly into the main perimeter.

The transition should appear naturally cut rather than separately styled.

Maintain balance between both sides while respecting the original

Avoid:

• curtain framing
• dramatic face framing

• heavy fringe

PERIMETER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain a soft continuous perimeter.

The perimeter should remain clearly readable without becoming blunt.

Use professional point-cutting logic.

Target approximately:

98% structural precision
2% natural microscopic variation

The edge should never resemble a ruler-straight digital line.

The perimeter should contain subtle natural variation created by:

• individual fibre termination
• strand grouping
• gravity
• microscopic point-cutting variation

The overall Lob outline must remain clearly visible.

ENDS

The perimeter should finish with a softly diffused edge.

The final 1–2 cm gradually releases a small amount of visual weight.

Individual ribbon groups may terminate at subtly different positions.

This creates microscopic natural irregularity while preserving a
clearly recognizable Lob silhouette.

Do not create:

• wispy ends
• transparent ends
• excessive tapering
• feathered shag-like ends
• broken perimeter

The edge remains soft but structurally controlled.

Neighbouring strand groups may:

• separate slightly
• overlap
• rejoin

Avoid:

• chunky strand separation
• repeated strand patterns
• editorial texture
• excessive flyaways
• artificial piece-y styling
• plastic smoothness

SOFT LOB

→ collarbone-to-shoulder length
→ softened perimeter
→ invisible internal layering
→ gentle face framing
→ medium density
→ natural movement

BLUNT LOB

→ stronger architectural perimeter
→ heavier lower edge
→ minimal internal movement
→ more rigid visual structure

ITALIAN BOB

→ shorter
→ denser
→ more compact
→ stronger rounded blowout

FRENCH BOB

→ shorter
→ more compact
→ lighter/breathable perimeter

BUTTERFLY CUT

→ stronger visible layer hierarchy
→ substantial face framing
→ cascading movement

LONG LAYERS

→ longer overall length
→ clearly visible cascading layers
→ more pronounced vertical movement

The Soft Lob must remain visually distinct from each.

SELF-CORRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If the result reads as a Long Bob:

→ preserve Lob length.
→ introduce subtle invisible internal layering.
→ soften the perimeter.

If the result reads as a Blunt Lob:

→ soften the perimeter.
→ increase microscopic point-cut variation.
→ reduce architectural rigidity.

If the result reads as a Butterfly Cut:

→ reduce visible layer hierarchy.
→ reconnect the layers into the Lob silhouette.
→ reduce dramatic face framing.

If the result reads as Long Layers:

→ reduce visible cascading structure.
→ preserve the continuous Lob perimeter.

If the result reads as Italian Bob:

→ reduce density.
→ lengthen the silhouette.
→ remove rounded blowout behaviour.

If the result becomes too flat:

→ introduce subtle natural movement through fibre behaviour,
not artificial volume.

If the result becomes too textured:

→ restore the unified Lob silhouette.
