# Soft Layered Bob — Production Notes

STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A short, rounded layered bob with:

• chin-to-slightly-below-chin length
• controlled crown
• soft internal layering
• diffused perimeter
• natural inward movement

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The style sits between the Soft Layered French Bob and longer
layered-bob structures.

French Bob:

• shorter
• more Parisian
• lighter
• more breathable

Soft Layered Bob:

• slightly longer
• rounded
• balanced
• clearly layered
• conventional soft-bob architecture

Italian Bob:

• heavier
• denser
• more polished
• stronger weight

PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. FRENCH BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• too short
• overly compact
• excessive Parisian character

Correction:

Allow slightly more length below chin and restore layered-bob structure.

2. ITALIAN BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive density
• heavy weight
• polished blowout
• strong perimeter

Correction:

Reduce weight and restore soft layering.

3. BLUNT BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• hard perimeter
• no internal movement
• ruler-straight edge

Correction:

Diffuse the perimeter and restore layered movement.

4. LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive length
• shoulder/collarbone proximity

Correction:

Shorten the entire lower perimeter.

5. BIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• insufficient lower weight
• overly short sides
• pixie-like silhouette

Correction:

Restore short-bob length and lower structure.

6. HELMET DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive crown volume
• rounded artificial shell

Correction:

Make crown and sides follow the skull.
