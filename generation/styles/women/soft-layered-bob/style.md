# Soft Layered Bob

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Soft Layered Bob: a short chin-to-slightly-below-chin bob with a rounded balanced silhouette, visible but blended internal layering, softened perimeter and natural inward movement. “Soft” is the finish; the haircut remains structurally a bob.

### LENGTH / PERIMETER
- Dominant perimeter sits at chin to slightly below chin.
- No strand reaches shoulder or collarbone.
- Maintain enough lower density to read as a bob, but diffuse the edge with subtle point-cut variation.
- Do not create a lob, micro bob or pixie-length silhouette.

### INTERNAL LAYER ARCHITECTURE
- Use three functional zones: controlled crown, mid-length internal layering, softened ends.
- Layering begins through the mid-lengths and subtly reduces weight toward the perimeter.
- Transitions are continuous and blended; no shelves, disconnected panels or obvious shag steps.
- Crown remains close to the skull with modest natural movement.

### SHAPE / WEIGHT
- Balanced rounded bob silhouette, with moderate weight through the sides and lower perimeter.
- Internal layers create movement without destroying the coherent bob outline.
- Ends may turn inward naturally from haircut weight, not from exaggerated styling.

### FACE FRAMING
- Use restrained cheekbone-to-jaw face framing only where needed to soften the front.
- Framing remains integrated into the bob and never becomes curtain bangs, butterfly wings or long isolated pieces.

### TEXTURE / FINISH
Soft, touchable, lightly diffused texture with fine strand grouping and natural inward settling. No fluffy blowout, beach waves, chunky texture or plastic polish.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Soft Layered Bob = chin bob length + moderate internal layers + softened perimeter + controlled rounded shape.
Distinct from Layered Bob by a more diffused edge and gentler movement; Structured Blunt Bob by less rigid perimeter; French Bob by less compact/Parisian styling; Soft Lob by materially shorter length; Shaggy Bob by cleaner internal structure.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No lob, long bob, micro bob, pixie, bixie, Italian bob, French bob, structured blunt bob, shaggy bob, shag, wolf cut, butterfly cut, heavy graduation, visible step layers, disconnected layers, curtain bangs, dramatic face framing, helmet shape, mushroom volume, excessive blowout, beach waves, wet look, plastic hair, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Soft Layered Bob.
