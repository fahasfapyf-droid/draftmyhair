# Soft Layered Bob

Create a true Soft Layered Bob: a compact chin-to-slightly-below-chin bob with moderate internal layering, a softened perimeter and controlled rounded movement. “Soft” describes the finish, not a loss of bob structure.

## LENGTH / PERIMETER
- Dominant perimeter sits at chin to slightly below-chin level.
- No dominant strand reaches shoulder or collarbone.
- Retain enough lower density to keep an unmistakable bob silhouette.
- Soften the edge with subtle point-cut variation rather than destroying the perimeter.
- Keep the silhouette balanced and rounded without mushroom or helmet volume.

## INTERNAL LAYER ARCHITECTURE
- Use three functional zones: controlled crown, moderate mid-length layering, softened perimeter.
- Begin internal weight release through the mid-lengths rather than creating short crown steps.
- Layer transitions remain continuous and blended.
- Internal movement must be clearly more developed than Classic/Blunt/Structured Blunt Bob, but cleaner and less fragmented than Shaggy Bob.

## WEIGHT / SHAPE
Maintain moderate side and lower weight. Internal layers create movement without collapsing the bob outline. Ends may settle inward naturally from haircut weight, not from exaggerated styling.

## FACE FRAMING
Use restrained cheekbone-to-jaw framing only where it supports the bob. It must merge directly into the side structure and never become curtain bangs, butterfly wings or long isolated pieces.

## TEXTURE / FINISH
Soft, touchable, lightly diffused texture with fine strand grouping and natural inward settling. Avoid fluffy blowout volume, beach waves, chunky texture or plastic polish.

## STYLE DIFFERENTIATION
Soft Layered Bob = chin bob length + moderate internal layers + softened perimeter + rounded controlled shape.
Distinct from Layered Bob by a more diffused perimeter and gentler movement; from Structured Blunt Bob by reduced edge rigidity; from Soft Layered French Bob by less compact Parisian character; from Soft Lob by materially shorter length; from Shaggy Bob by cleaner internal structure.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No lob, long bob, micro bob, pixie, bixie, French bob, Italian bob, structured blunt bob, shaggy bob, shag, wolf cut, butterfly cut, heavy graduation, visible step layers, disconnected layers, curtain bangs, dramatic face framing, helmet shape, mushroom volume, excessive blowout, beach waves, wet look, plastic hair or wig effect.
