## Overall

Overall is a calibrated transformation-quality summary, not the weakest single dimension.

Production readiness requires every applicable transformation dimension and root integration to meet the production floor, plus all hard gates. A strong overall average cannot rescue a failed production floor.

## Italian Bob Visual Anchors

- Compact jaw-length bob; not a lob.
- Clearly rounded, compact silhouette.
- Substantial internal jaw-level weight.
- Controlled inward blowout/contour.
- Balanced left/right shape.
- Clean, believable perimeter.
- Natural scalp/root integration.
- No wig-like edges, painted-on hair, or transformation spill.

Aesthetic preference is not a defect. Score observable compliance with these anchors rather than generic attractiveness.
