# Italian Bob

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE INFORMATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Style Name: Italian Bob

Category: Women's Haircut

Length: Short

Difficulty: High

Primary Characteristic:

A premium salon-quality compact jaw-length bob distinguished by dense internal weight, a rounded silhouette, a refined controlled perimeter, a deep natural side part, and a polished inward blowout that creates an unmistakably luxurious Italian Bob.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create an authentic modern Italian Bob while preserving the subject's natural head proportions and photographic identity.

The finished hairstyle must immediately and unmistakably read as a true Italian Bob rather than a French Bob, blunt bob, soft lob, layered bob, generic chin-length haircut, or preserved long hairstyle.

This is a true haircut transformation.

When the source contains long hair, remove the existing long hair mass and replace it with the compact Italian Bob structure defined below.

Do not preserve long or shoulder-length sections merely because they exist in the source photograph.

The final hairstyle must be clearly shorter than the original long hairstyle whenever the source begins longer than jaw length.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIRCUT TRANSFORMATION GEOMETRY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The defining transformation is a compact jaw-length hair mass.

Create one coherent bob perimeter terminating around the jawline to upper-neck region according to the subject's actual anatomy.

All visible hair belonging to the finished haircut must conform to the new compact bob geometry.

Do not leave:

• long rear sections
• shoulder-length sections
• long side panels
• disconnected long pieces
• residual original-length strands
• hidden long hair extending beneath the bob
• a lob-length foundation

The original long hairstyle must not remain underneath or around the new bob.

The haircut itself must create the shortened silhouette.

Do not simulate shortness by merely folding, sweeping, tucking, or hiding long hair.

The perimeter must remain visibly compact from the source camera angle.

SOURCE HAIR LENGTH DOES NOT OVERRIDE THE REQUESTED ITALIAN BOB GEOMETRY.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PARTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a clearly visible deep natural side part.

The part should be substantially off-center rather than a middle or center part.

The part should originate naturally near the crown and direct the front hair diagonally toward one side.

The side part must be created by the haircut's natural root direction and hair distribution, not by placing a superficial sweep over an unchanged middle part.

Do not preserve a center part when creating the finished Italian Bob.

Do not create curtain bangs or a curtain-parted structure.

The side part should remain elegant and controlled, not exaggerated or theatrical.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BOB SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a dense, luxurious bob with a visually strong rounded silhouette.

The haircut should terminate naturally around the jawline while maintaining concentrated visual weight through the lower half of the hairstyle.

The silhouette must be compact rather than elongated.

The result must not read as a lob.

The overall appearance should feel expensive, polished, and structurally refined without appearing overly sculpted or artificial.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERALL LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain a true jaw-length bob.

The primary perimeter should finish approximately at the jawline, with only minor anatomical variation toward the upper neck where required by the subject's proportions.

The finished hairstyle must remain clearly above shoulder length.

Front sections may fall only slightly longer through natural movement, but must remain within the compact bob silhouette.

Do not create pronounced A-line geometry.

Do not create a soft lob.

Do not preserve the original long-hair length.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain high internal density throughout the haircut.

Visual weight should remain concentrated through the lower half of the bob while gradually becoming lighter toward the perimeter.

The hairstyle should feel substantial without becoming bulky.

Do not hollow out the interior.

Do not create excessive visible layering.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a refined salon perimeter with continuous visual weight.

The lower edge should remain controlled and approximately jaw length while displaying extremely subtle natural variation created by professional point-cutting.

Individual strand groups should terminate at slightly different lengths while preserving the compact overall silhouette.

The perimeter must read as a genuine haircut edge rather than a digital mask.

Avoid perfectly straight or mechanically uniform edges.

Do not allow isolated long strands to break the jaw-length silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INTERNAL STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain dense internal structure with minimal visible layering.

The interior should appear full, cohesive, and naturally supported.

Avoid hollow interiors, excessive texturizing, or visible graduation.

The haircut should preserve the characteristic weight associated with a premium Italian Bob.

The internal structure must support the compact silhouette rather than extending the hairstyle downward.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SURFACE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The exterior surface should appear smooth, polished, and naturally reflective.

Hair should resolve into thousands of individual fibres while maintaining a continuous premium finish.

Avoid plastic-looking surfaces or overly uniform strand patterns.

Natural strand variation should remain visible throughout the haircut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MOVEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Movement should remain subtle and controlled.

The hairstyle is defined by structure rather than texture.

Allow delicate natural movement near the perimeter while preserving the overall rounded silhouette.

The haircut should remain immediately recognizable even when perfectly still.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ENDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Finish the perimeter using refined salon point-cutting techniques.

Create microscopic natural variation across neighbouring strand groups.

Avoid blunt artificial edges, shredded texture, heavy feathering, or aggressive texturizing.

The ends must remain compact and must not extend into a lob or long-hair silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FINISH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Finish with a premium salon blowout.

The ends should naturally curve inward beneath the jaw using the weight of the haircut rather than excessive styling.

The inward bend should appear soft, natural, and created with a professional round-brush blowout.

Avoid visible curling-iron styling or exaggerated inward curls.

The blowout must enhance the bob geometry rather than conceal an unchanged long hairstyle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ROOT AND SCALP INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Roots must emerge naturally from the existing hairline with realistic density and directional flow.

Preserve the source hairline architecture.

The deep side part must originate naturally from the scalp and transition continuously into the new bob structure.

Do not regenerate the scalp or alter non-hair anatomy to accommodate the haircut.

The newly shortened hair must integrate with the existing scalp, lighting, texture, and photographic grain.

• dense premium weight
• rounded silhouette
• clearly visible deep side part
• refined controlled perimeter
• polished inward blowout
• compact luxury proportions

If the result still reads as long hair, a lob, or an unchanged source hairstyle, the Italian Bob transformation is incomplete.
