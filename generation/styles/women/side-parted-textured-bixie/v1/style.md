# SIDE-PARTED TEXTURED BIXIE
# Draft My Hair Style Engine — v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE TARGET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a genuine modern side-parted textured bixie.

The hairstyle must immediately read as a bixie rather than:

• bob
• pixie
• shag
• short layered bob
• modern fluffy pixie

The defining character is the hybrid relationship between:

PIXIE-LIKE COMPACTNESS
+
SHORT-BOB-LIKE SOFT MOVEMENT

The result should feel:

• modern
• effortless
• feminine
• compact
• naturally lived-in
• professionally cut
• softly textured

The hairstyle must remain unmistakably short from every viewing angle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Overall length should sit between the upper cheekbone and just above the jaw.

Nothing should extend below the jaw.

The nape remains short.

The haircut must retain compact proportions.

Do not allow the lower sections to accumulate enough length to become a bob.

The overall length must remain clearly shorter than a conventional bob.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Weight distribution is the primary defining structural feature.

Concentrate visual weight around:

• temples
• upper cheekbones
• upper sides

Allow the weight to gradually dissolve toward:

• lower sides
• area below the ears
• nape

The lower perimeter must remain visually light.

It must never resemble a continuous bob outline.

The haircut should feel heavier above the ears and lighter below them.

This weight distribution must distinguish the bixie from a short textured bob.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PARTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a clear natural side part.

The side part establishes the overall flow.

The heavier side should carry only slightly more visual weight.

Do not create dramatic asymmetry.

The asymmetry must feel organic rather than intentionally designed.

The part should appear naturally formed by the haircut rather than artificially drawn.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a compact silhouette.

Keep the haircut close to the skull.

Allow slight natural asymmetry.

Do not widen the lower half.

Avoid:

• rounded bob silhouette
• mushroom effect
• crown bubble
• helmet shape
• excessive lower fullness

The silhouette should hug the skull through the upper structure before softly releasing through the layered mid-section.

The shape should remain compact rather than expanded.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LAYER STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Short internal layering begins around the crown.

Primary layering is concentrated through:

• crown
• temples
• upper sides

Lower sections progressively become lighter.

Layering should sculpt the haircut rather than create obvious visible steps.

The layers must blend naturally into one another.

Avoid:

• visible step layers
• disconnected sections
• abrupt layer breaks
• heavy lower layering
• shag architecture

The haircut should remain structurally controlled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain controlled crown volume.

The crown should remain compact and close to the skull.

Do not create:

• crown bubble
• inflated volume
• airy blowout
• exaggerated lift

Subtle natural separation is acceptable.

The crown should support the bixie silhouette without becoming a modern fluffy pixie.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRONT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Front pieces should emerge naturally from the side part.

Allow the front sections to softly cross the forehead.

They must NOT become a conventional side fringe.

Avoid:

• curtain fringe
• deliberate diagonal sweep
• dramatic side bang
• heavily styled fringe

The shortest front pieces may lightly graze the forehead.

The front should appear naturally settled rather than deliberately arranged.

Maintain slight natural irregularity.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AROUND THE EARS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair should naturally contour around both ears.

Do not deliberately clear the ears.

Do not create bulky side coverage.

Do not hide the ears beneath excessive hair.

The hair should follow the ear contour with slight natural overlap.

The relationship between hair and ears should appear incidental and naturally produced by the haircut.

Avoid an aggressively exposed-ear pixie treatment.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The lower perimeter must never form a continuous bob line.

Individual small clusters should terminate at slightly different heights.

Create naturally interrupted layered exits.

Avoid:

• horizontal bob line
• blunt perimeter
• uniform lower edge
• mathematically even termination

The perimeter should remain soft and fragmented through natural layering.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Texture must emerge from the haircut architecture rather than artificial styling.

Create:

• fine strand definition
• subtle irregular strand grouping
• natural micro-separation
• restrained fibre variation
• quiet movement

Avoid:

• chunky texture
• excessive piece separation
• repeated strand patterns
• exaggerated flyaways
• editorial styling
• artificial piece-y finish
• crunchy texture
• wet appearance

The texture should remain believable at close inspection.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair follows natural growth direction.

Allow subtle inconsistencies in strand direction.

Avoid mirrored flow.

Avoid repeated curvature.

Avoid identical strand trajectories.

Avoid overly organized strand groupings.

The hairstyle should appear casually settled after ordinary wear.

Movement should be:

• quiet
• gravity-driven
• natural
• slightly irregular

Do not make the hairstyle look deliberately sculpted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STRAND BEHAVIOUR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair should resolve into realistic individual fibres and small fibre groups.

Neighbouring groups should vary subtly in:

• orientation
• density
• overlap
• length
• reflection

Some strands may merge into larger groups.

Some may separate slightly.

Avoid uniform digital strand patterns.

Avoid perfectly parallel strands.

Avoid broad artificial hair surfaces.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ROOT & HAIRLINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair must emerge naturally from the existing scalp.

Maintain:

• natural root emergence
• realistic density
• subtle temple transitions
• slight natural hairline irregularity
• microscopic hairline fray

The scalp-to-hair transition must remain organic.

Avoid a sharp artificial boundary.

Avoid painted-on roots.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VOLUME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Volume follows the natural skull.

No artificial lift.

No airy modern blowout.

No sculpted crown.

No exaggerated fullness.

No expansion of the head silhouette.

The haircut should remain compact and lightly weighted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEIGHT RELEASE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The upper structure carries the majority of the haircut's visual weight.

As the hair approaches:

• below the ears
• lower sides
• nape

the visual density and weight should gradually reduce.

This release should be subtle.

Do not create obvious thinning.

Do not create transparent sections.

The purpose is to prevent the haircut from becoming bob-like.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BIXIE VS PIXIE CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut must retain enough side and front length to distinguish it from a conventional pixie.

If the result becomes too pixie-like:

→ restore subtle length around the upper sides
→ restore soft layered movement
→ maintain cheekbone-level weight
→ preserve the short-bob influence

Do not solve this by adding excessive volume.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BIXIE VS BOB CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut must remain clearly shorter and lighter than a bob.

If the result becomes too bob-like:

→ shorten lower sections
→ fragment the perimeter
→ reduce lower weight
→ tighten the nape
→ maintain stronger upper-side weight

Never create a continuous bob perimeter.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REALISM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair must grow naturally from the scalp.

Maintain:

• realistic density
• natural gravity
• believable fibre behaviour
• authentic root integration
• original lighting response
• original photographic grain
• original sharpness

The hairstyle should look as though it was cut by a skilled professional stylist.

The final image must read as a real photograph.

No AI-generated hair appearance.

No wig effect.

No painted hair.

No plastic surface.

STYLE RECOGNITION TEST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before completing the image, evaluate the haircut as an experienced stylist.

The result must immediately read:

SIDE-PARTED TEXTURED BIXIE

If it resembles a bob:

→ reduce lower length
→ break the lower perimeter
→ reduce lower weight

If it resembles a pixie:

→ restore cheekbone-level side weight
→ restore soft movement
→ preserve slightly longer front and upper-side sections

If it resembles a shag:

→ reduce visible layering
→ reduce texture
→ restore compact structure

If it resembles a modern fluffy pixie:

→ reduce crown volume
→ reduce piece-y separation
→ tighten the silhouette

FINAL VISUAL OBJECTIVE

The final hairstyle should look like a professionally executed modern side-parted textured bixie photographed naturally.
