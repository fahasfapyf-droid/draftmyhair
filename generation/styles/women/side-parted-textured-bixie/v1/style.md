# Side-Parted Textured Bixie

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true Side-Parted Textured Bixie: compact pixie-bob hybrid proportions + genuine side part + short layered movement + light interrupted lower perimeter.

### BIXIE GEOMETRY
- Overall length ranges from upper cheekbone through jaw, with a clearly shorter nape.
- Upper sides/front retain enough length for bob influence; nothing reaches lob length.
- Lower perimeter is light and interrupted, never a continuous bob line.
- Upper structure carries more weight than below the ears.

### SIDE PART
- Clear off-center root part from frontal hairline through crown.
- Slightly heavier side; organic asymmetry only.
- No painted line, extreme deep part or dramatic side bang.

### LAYERS / TEXTURE
- Controlled internal layers through crown, temples and upper sides.
- Fine irregular strand grouping; no chunky shag steps.
- Lower weight progressively releases toward nape.
- Crown stays compact with modest natural lift.

### EARS / FRONT
Hair naturally contours around existing ears; no carved openings. Front follows the part and may lightly cross the forehead, but does not become conventional side fringe or curtain fringe.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Side-Parted Textured Bixie = bixie proportions + side part + compact layered structure + textured/light lower perimeter.
Distinct from Pixie, Bob, Side Part alone, Shag and Wolf Cut.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No conventional pixie, long bob, lob, blunt bob, continuous bob line, shag, wolf cut, modern shag, mullet, undercut, bowl cut, dramatic side bang, curtain bangs, deep theatrical part, crown bubble, excessive volume, chunky texture, wet look, plastic shine, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Side-Parted Textured Bixie.
