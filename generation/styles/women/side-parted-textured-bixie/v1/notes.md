# Side-Parted Textured Bixie — Production Notes

Status: v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A modern side-parted textured bixie.

The style occupies the space between a pixie and short bob.

The hairstyle must retain characteristics of both without becoming either.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The most important structural relationship is:

UPPER WEIGHT
↓
CHEEKBONE / TEMPLE AREA
↓
GRADUAL WEIGHT RELEASE
↓
LIGHTER LOWER PERIMETER
↓
SHORT NAPE

This prevents the haircut from becoming bob-like.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY STYLE RISKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. BOB DRIFT

The lower perimeter becomes too continuous.

Correction:

Reduce lower length and fragment the perimeter.

2. PIXIE DRIFT

The sides become too short and the cheekbone weight disappears.

Correction:

Restore upper-side length and soft movement.

3. SHAG DRIFT

Layering becomes too visible and texture dominates.

Correction:

Reduce layering and texture.

4. MODERN FLUFFY PIXIE DRIFT

Crown becomes too voluminous.

Correction:

Reduce crown lift and tighten the silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Texture supports the haircut.

Texture must never become the primary visual feature.

The haircut should still read correctly if texture is reduced.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PARTING RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The side part should be clear but natural.

Do not create an exaggerated fashion side sweep.
