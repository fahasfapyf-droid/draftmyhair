# Middle Part

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY

Modify only the hair. The face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Any natural head tilt in the source is intentional and must remain unchanged. Jaw shape and width are locked. Chin is locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. Match source scale and framing exactly; no zoom, crop or reframe.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION — CRITICAL

Preserve exact facial and visible skin texture, pores, tone and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beauty filter, retouching, relighting or skin-tone drift. Hair must not cast artificial color onto skin. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY

STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, sampling hairline, temples, part and nape where visible.

STEP 2 — Ignore and discard color from background, lighting, environment, clothing and reflections.

STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact source root color and only natural variation already present. Do not invent highlights, lowlights or dye.

STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: would the reproduced hair color still look correct on a pure white background? If no, correct the contamination.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Create a true Middle Part defined by a deliberate, clean central parting that visibly divides the hair into balanced left and right sections. This is a parting architecture, not a new haircut length.

### PARTING GEOMETRY
- Establish one clear part beginning at the natural frontal hairline and continuing backward along the central top of the head.
- Keep the part visually centered relative to the skull, while respecting the photographed head position rather than rotating or re-centering the head.
- The two sides should emerge from the part with broadly balanced visual mass and natural asymmetry in individual strands.
- The part must be created by actual directional separation at the roots, not by painting a dark line onto the scalp.
- Maintain believable scalp visibility along the part without widening it unnaturally.

### LENGTH AND SILHOUETTE
- Preserve the intended source-compatible overall haircut length unless a minimal adjustment is necessary to make the central part coherent.
- Do not convert the style into a bob, lob, layers, bangs or a specific haircut category.
- Allow each side to fall according to its existing length, texture and gravity.
- Keep the crown controlled and prevent artificial volume from developing on either side of the part.

### FRONT AND SIDE FLOW
- Direct the left and right front sections naturally away from the center part and back toward the temples and sides.
- Keep the forehead open unless the source haircut already contains a fringe; do not invent curtain bangs or a new fringe.
- Preserve realistic temple transitions and ear relationships.
- Individual strands may cross slightly within each side, but the central division must remain readable.

### FINISH
Use natural root separation, realistic strand grouping, subtle density variation and gravity-driven fall. The part should look created by normal professional styling rather than a hard geometric line. Avoid excessive symmetry, glossy styling or a salon blowout.

### DIFFERENTIATION
The defining combination is a genuine central root part + balanced left/right directional flow + preserved haircut identity. It must remain distinct from Curtains, Curtain Bangs, Side Part, Deep Side Part and artificial painted scalp lines.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No side part, deep side part, zig-zag part, off-center part, artificial painted scalp line, curtain bangs, invented fringe, forced symmetrical hair, exaggerated crown volume, blowout, wet styling, haircut-length drift, bob conversion, lob conversion, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The finished image must look like the same original photograph after professional styling into a clean Middle Part, not a regenerated person or synthetic wig.
