# Bowl Cut

Create a true modern bowl cut defined by a continuous rounded upper silhouette and a controlled lower perimeter.

## BOWL ARCHITECTURE

- Build a continuous bowl-like contour around the head rather than a generic rounded bob.
- Maintain substantial, even upper hair mass that follows the fixed skull geometry.
- The lower perimeter must sit visibly higher than a conventional bob, around the brow, temple, or ear-region according to the intended modern variation.
- Keep the upper mass and lower perimeter structurally connected as one haircut.
- The contour should remain clearly bowl-shaped without becoming a spherical helmet.

## FRINGE AND PERIMETER

- Integrate the front fringe directly into the continuous bowl silhouette.
- The fringe must connect naturally into the side perimeter rather than appearing as a separate bang block.
- Keep the lower perimeter controlled and deliberate, with subtle natural strand irregularity.
- Do not taper the upper mass into a generic crop or disconnect the fringe from the bowl structure.

## TEXTURE AND VOLUME

- Maintain controlled rounded volume that follows the skull rather than floating above it.
- Roots must emerge naturally with realistic density and directional flow.
- Use believable strand grouping, occlusion, and micro-variation.
- Preserve photographic lighting and natural hair texture; no artificial helmet surface.

## STYLE RECOGNITION

The result must immediately read as a modern bowl cut through its continuous rounded contour, integrated fringe, substantial upper mass, and visibly elevated lower perimeter.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No pageboy, mushroom haircut, generic rounded bob, pixie, crop, helmet-like volume, disconnected fringe, exaggerated crown, lob, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
