# Layered Bob

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY

Modify only the hair. The face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Any natural head tilt in the source is intentional and must remain unchanged. Jaw shape and width are locked. Chin is locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. Match source scale and framing exactly; no zoom, crop or reframe.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION — CRITICAL

Preserve exact facial and visible skin texture, pores, tone and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beauty filter, retouching, relighting or skin-tone drift. Hair must not cast artificial color onto skin. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY

STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, sampling hairline, temples, part and nape where visible.

STEP 2 — Ignore and discard color from background, lighting, environment, clothing and reflections.

STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact source root color and only natural variation already present. Do not invent highlights, lowlights or dye.

STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: would the reproduced hair color still look correct on a pure white background? If no, correct the contamination.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Create a true Layered Bob as a compact bob-length haircut whose identity comes from visible internal layering while retaining a recognizable bob perimeter.

### LENGTH AND PERIMETER
- Keep the overall length within classic bob territory, around chin to upper-neck level, clearly shorter than a lob.
- Maintain a coherent lower perimeter with moderate weight rather than a razor-thin outline.
- The perimeter may be softly diffused by layering, but it must still read as a deliberate bob shape.

### INTERNAL LAYERS
- Create several controlled internal layers through the crown, upper sides and lower side sections.
- The layers must progressively shorten toward the upper structure while remaining integrated into the bob foundation.
- Use enough internal separation to be visibly more layered than Classic Bob, Blunt Bob or Structured Blunt Bob.
- Do not create the short, feathered fragmentation of a shag.

### SILHOUETTE AND FINISH
Keep the crown controlled and the overall silhouette compact. Allow natural movement and slight inward/outward variation at different layer levels, with realistic strand grouping and believable weight transitions.

### DIFFERENTIATION
The defining combination is bob length + recognizable perimeter + deliberate internal layering. It must sit structurally between a classic bob and a shag, while remaining distinct from Soft Layered Bob, Shaggy Bob, French Bob and Italian Bob.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No classic bob with only styling movement, blunt bob, structured blunt bob, French bob, Italian bob, soft lob, shaggy bob, shag, wolf cut, butterfly cut, heavy choppy layers, pixie, bixie, long layered haircut, disconnected shelves, exaggerated crown volume, artificial wig effect, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The finished image must look like the same original photograph after a professional haircut, not a regenerated person or synthetic wig.
