# Wolf Cut Reference

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Wolf Cut is a modern layered haircut that combines aggressive internal layering with preserved overall length.

Unlike traditional layered haircuts, the Wolf Cut creates two visually distinct structural zones while maintaining a continuous natural silhouette.

Its identity comes from haircut architecture rather than styling.
