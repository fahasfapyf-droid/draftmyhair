# Wolf Cut

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE INFORMATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Style Name: Wolf Cut

Category: Women's Haircut

Length: Medium to Long

Difficulty: Very High

Primary Characteristic:

A modern Wolf Cut defined by aggressive internal layering, pronounced structural separation between the upper and lower sections, preserved overall length, and naturally fragmented texture that creates effortless movement without increasing the external silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create an authentic modern Wolf Cut through haircut architecture rather than styling.

The finished hairstyle must immediately read as a genuine Wolf Cut while preserving the original overall hair length and external silhouette.

The hairstyle should be instantly distinguishable from a Butterfly Cut, Shag, Mullet, or heavily layered long haircut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain the original overall external silhouette while transforming the internal haircut structure.

The hairstyle should appear slim, elongated, and naturally fragmented.

Internal complexity should increase significantly while the external outline remains balanced and close to the original profile.

The crown should closely follow the natural skull shape without introducing additional height or visual expansion.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERALL LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve nearly the entire original hair length.

The longest layers should remain continuously visible beneath the upper structure.

The Wolf Cut is created through internal restructuring rather than shortening the overall hairstyle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LAYER ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Construct the hairstyle around two clearly distinguishable structural zones.

The upper structure should contain aggressive internal layering with substantial weight removal.

The lower foundation should remain long, continuous, and comparatively dense.

These two structural systems should blend naturally while remaining visually independent.

Transitions should appear organic without forming shelves, steps, or disconnected sections.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UPPER STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The upper section should begin around the crown and extend through the cheekbone-to-jaw region.

Layers should terminate at varying lengths to create natural fragmentation and movement.

The upper structure should feel light, irregular, and dynamic while maintaining professional salon balance.

Movement should originate from the haircut itself rather than styling.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LOWER FOUNDATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain a long, stable lower foundation beneath the upper layers.

The lower section should provide visual weight and continuity while allowing the upper layers to remain clearly distinguishable.

This preserved foundation is a defining characteristic of an authentic Wolf Cut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Remove substantial internal weight throughout the upper section while preserving the overall external mass of the hairstyle.

Weight should gradually transition into the denser lower foundation without abrupt changes.

The hairstyle should never appear bulky, inflated, or excessively voluminous.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The perimeter should appear naturally irregular.

Individual layer groups should terminate independently while maintaining a cohesive overall outline.

Avoid blunt edges, perfectly symmetrical finishes, or repetitive strand termination.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ENDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every structural layer should finish independently.

Neighbouring strand groups should terminate at subtly different positions, creating hundreds of microscopic irregularities throughout the perimeter.

The overall finish should resemble an expensive salon haircut that has settled naturally after several days of wear.

Avoid uniform feathering, rhythmic spacing, or mechanically repeated strand endings.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIR PHYSICS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair should behave as thousands of individual fibres rather than grouped locks.

Create irregular ribbon-like strand groups that naturally separate, overlap, and reunite throughout the hairstyle.

The upper third should display noticeably greater fragmentation than the lower foundation.

Natural overlap between short and long layers should generate subtle internal depth and shadow without increasing overall density.

Texture should appear as a natural consequence of the haircut rather than deliberate styling.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ROOT INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain the original hairline.

Hair should emerge naturally from the scalp with realistic density and authentic root transitions.

Preserve subtle irregularities around the temples and hairline to maintain photographic realism.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE RECOGNITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Within one second of viewing, the hairstyle should be unmistakably recognizable as a modern Wolf Cut.

The viewer should immediately perceive:

• aggressive internal layering

• clear upper and lower structural separation

• preserved overall length

• naturally fragmented texture

• effortless movement

• authentic salon craftsmanship

The hairstyle must immediately communicate a professionally executed Wolf Cut rather than a Butterfly Cut, Shag, Mullet, or generic layered haircut.
