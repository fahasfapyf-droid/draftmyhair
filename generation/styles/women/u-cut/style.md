# U-Cut

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a true U-Cut defined by a smooth U-shaped rear perimeter. The center-back is the shortest point of the lower outline, with both sides gradually lengthening in a continuous curve toward the front.

### PERIMETER GEOMETRY
- Center-back establishes the bottom of the U.
- Length increases continuously and symmetrically from center-back through rear corners, sides and toward the front.
- The transition must be smooth and broad, never a sharp V point.
- The front remains longer than the center-back but must not become a disconnected pair of face-framing panels.

### LENGTH / WEIGHT
- Preserve a medium-to-long foundation appropriate to a U-cut.
- Retain enough lower weight for the U perimeter to remain visually obvious.
- Do not over-layer the lower edge until the U disappears.
- Internal layers, if present, remain subordinate to the perimeter geometry.

### SYMMETRY / FLOW
- Left and right sides follow the same U trajectory while retaining microscopic natural fibre variation.
- Hair settles naturally under gravity.
- No asymmetrical haircut effect.

### TEXTURE / FINISH
Use smooth natural strand grouping with restrained movement. The U should be visible through the mass and termination line, not painted as a graphic curve. Ends remain natural and slightly varied.

### DIFFERENTIATION
U-Cut = continuous rounded U perimeter with shortest center-back.
Distinct from V-Cut, which converges to a central point; C-Cut, which has a shallower C arc; straight blunt cuts, which lack the curved rear perimeter; and butterfly/shag cuts, where internal layer hierarchy dominates.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No V-cut, central pointed tail, sharp V, C-cut, straight blunt perimeter, mullet, wolf cut, shag, butterfly cut, jellyfish cut, disconnected front pieces, extreme face framing, asymmetrical hemline, excessive layering, transparent ends, wet look, plastic shine, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed U-Cut.
