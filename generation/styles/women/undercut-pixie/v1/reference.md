# Undercut Pixie Reference

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Undercut Pixie is a short haircut defined by a strong contrast between a substantially longer crown and an extremely short clipped region around the sides and nape.

Its identity comes primarily from the undercut architecture.

Texture and styling are secondary.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CORE ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut contains two primary visible length zones.

ZONE 1 — LONG CROWN

Approximately 3–4 inches.

The crown remains substantially longer than the lower region.

ZONE 2 — BUZZED UNDERCUT

Approximately #0–#1 clipper equivalent.

The clipped region remains extremely short and close to the scalp.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UNDERCUT PLANE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The clipped region should form one continuous anatomical plane.

It begins below the temple region.

It continues above and around the ears.

It continues through the nape.

The clipped region should not appear as isolated patches.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TRANSITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The defining transition is an abrupt reduction in hair length.

The haircut does not rely on a fade.

The contrast should be created by:

long crown

versus

very short undercut.

Tiny natural irregularities are acceptable.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crown provides the visible personality of the haircut.

It should retain:

• natural length
• textured grouping
• subtle movement
• layer overlap
• localized compression
• natural overhang

The crown should project naturally beyond the undercut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERHANG
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crown should create a subtle overhanging ledge above the clipped region.

The overhang should not form a perfectly straight horizontal or vertical boundary.

Individual strands may overlap the transition naturally.

This helps prevent a synthetic cutout appearance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BUZZED REGION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The clipped area should display:

• near-scalp visibility
• microscopic follicle direction
• natural density variation
• authentic clipper grain
• subtle tonal variation

It should never appear airbrushed or digitally painted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
DISTINCTION FROM OTHER STYLES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Classic Pixie

Short throughout the haircut.

Undercut Pixie

Long crown combined with extremely short clipped lower regions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Tapered Pixie

Uses gradual length reduction.

Undercut Pixie

Uses abrupt length contrast.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Bixie

Longer overall silhouette with bob/pixie hybrid proportions.

Undercut Pixie

Clearly shorter lower architecture with a dominant undercut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Fade

Contains multiple intermediate lengths.

Undercut Pixie

Contains two dominant visible zones without a gradual fade.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Within one second, the viewer should recognize:

• long textured crown
• extremely short undercut
• strong architectural contrast
• natural overhang
• modern salon execution

The undercut must be recognizable before the viewer notices the styling details.
