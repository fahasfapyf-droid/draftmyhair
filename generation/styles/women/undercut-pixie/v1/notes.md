KNOWN FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Normal tapered pixie

The undercut becomes too gradual.

2. Fade

The model introduces intermediate lengths.

3. Disconnected pixie

The crown becomes visually detached from the undercut.

4. Artificial buzz texture

The clipped area becomes plastic, airbrushed, or perfectly uniform.

5. Excessive crown styling

The crown becomes editorial or heavily sculpted instead of naturally worn.
