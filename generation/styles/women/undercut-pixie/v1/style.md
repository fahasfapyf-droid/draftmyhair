# Undercut Pixie

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine modern Undercut Pixie defined by a strong two-zone architecture: long textured crown over an extremely short clipped undercut. The undercut contrast is the primary identity.

### TWO-ZONE ARCHITECTURE
ZONE 1 — LONG CROWN:
- Dominant upper crown approximately 3–4 inches, depending on source scale.
- Textured, naturally overlapping and visibly longer than the lower zone.
- Controlled projection; never inflated.

ZONE 2 — CLIPPED UNDERCUT:
- Extremely short, approximately #0–#1 clipper appearance, with subtle scalp visibility.
- Continuous plane below the temple ridge, around the ears and through the nape.
- Consistent shortness throughout; no intermediate fade zone.

### TRANSITION
- Create an abrupt length reduction from crown to clipped region.
- Boundary is haircut-length contrast, not a fade.
- Allow tiny natural irregularities and occasional crown overlap.
- Never create a ruler-straight digital edge or gradual taper.

### CROWN / OVERHANG
- Crown naturally overhangs the clipped region and varies subtly around the head.
- Use real overlap, occlusion and shadow pockets for depth.
- Fine piece separation is controlled, not shredded.

### CLIPPED SURFACE
- Short hair retains authentic follicle direction, density variation and micro-grain.
- It must look like clipper-cut hair, not an airbrushed scalp or painted skin.
- Preserve natural temple, ear and nape irregularity.

### FINISH
Matte-to-soft-satin, naturally worn texture with realistic gravity and fibre behaviour. No wet, crunchy or editorial finish.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Undercut Pixie = long textured crown + extremely short continuous undercut + abrupt length contrast.
Distinct from Pixie/Disconnected Pixie by the clipped lower plane; from Fade by absence of gradual blending; from Undercut Bob by materially shorter pixie crown geometry.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No classic pixie, tapered pixie, bixie, bob, short crop, fade, taper, mohawk, faux hawk, shaved decorative channel, intermediate transition zone, soft fade, ruler-straight boundary, airbrushed scalp, plastic hair, wet look, crunchy styling, excessive crown volume, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Undercut Pixie.
