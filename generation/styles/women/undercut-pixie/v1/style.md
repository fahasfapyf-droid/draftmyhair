# Undercut Pixie

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE INFORMATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Style Name: Undercut Pixie

Category: Women's Haircut

Length: Short

Difficulty: Very High

Primary Characteristic:

A modern Undercut Pixie defined by strong contrast between a long textured crown and an extremely short clipped undercut surrounding the sides and nape.

The defining characteristic is the undercut architecture rather than the texture of the crown.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a genuine modern Undercut Pixie with a clearly visible two-zone structure.

The hairstyle must immediately read as an undercut before the viewer notices the pixie styling.

The finished haircut must never resemble a classic pixie, tapered pixie, bixie, disconnected pixie, short crop, fade, or short bob.

The visual identity must come from the abrupt reduction in hair length between the long crown and clipped lower region.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIRCUT ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Construct the haircut from exactly two primary visible length zones:

ZONE 1 — LONG CROWN

A substantially longer textured crown forming the dominant upper hair mass.

ZONE 2 — BUZZED UNDERCUT

An extremely short clipped region surrounding the lower sides and nape.

The contrast between these two zones must remain immediately visible.

Do not introduce a third intermediate length zone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UNDERCUT PLANE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The clipped region begins below the temple ridge.

Continue the undercut uninterrupted around both ears and through the nape.

The clipped section should behave as one continuous anatomical plane rather than separate buzzed patches.

The lower region should remain consistently short throughout its entire area.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UNDERCUT LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain an extremely short clipped appearance approximately equivalent to a #0–#1 clipper length.

The scalp should remain subtly visible through the short hair.

Preserve microscopic follicle direction and natural density variation.

The clipped surface should resemble authentic professional clipper work rather than an airbrushed scalp.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UNDERCUT TRANSITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create an immediate reduction in length between the long crown and clipped undercut.

The transition must be created by hair-length contrast rather than fading.

The boundary should appear professionally clipper-cut while retaining tiny natural irregularities.

Allow occasional isolated longer hairs to overlap the transition naturally.

Avoid:

• gradual blending
• tapering
• fade behavior
• soft transition zones
• ruler-straight digital boundaries

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain a clearly dominant crown approximately 3–4 inches in length.

The crown must be visibly longer than the undercut.

Allow the crown to fall naturally forward or slightly toward one side according to the haircut's natural behaviour.

The crown should visibly project beyond the clipped region.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create natural compression and localized settling throughout the crown.

Use subtle layer overlap and uneven elevation to create authentic structure.

Neighbouring sections should partially overlap and naturally occlude one another.

Create small shadow pockets between overlapping layers.

The architecture should come from the haircut itself rather than excessive styling.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERHANG
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The longer crown should naturally extend beyond the clipped undercut.

Create a subtle overhanging relationship above the transition.

The overhang should vary naturally around the sides of the head.

Some isolated crown strands may project beyond the clipped region before returning toward the longer hair mass.

The crown should never terminate flush against the transition line.

The separation between crown and undercut should come from overlapping hair mass rather than a perfectly vertical edge.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create natural strand grouping with fine separation between neighbouring sections.

The crown should appear piece-y without becoming excessively fragmented.

Maintain subtle flyaways and natural fibre variation.

Use a matte-to-soft-satin surface finish.

Hair should appear naturally worn rather than wet, crunchy, heavily styled, or editorial.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL DEPTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create multiple overlapping depth planes within the crown.

Foreground strands should partially obscure deeper sections.

Some strand groups should disappear beneath neighbouring layers before naturally re-emerging.

Create depth primarily through occlusion and layer overlap rather than artificial volume.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VOLUME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain controlled crown volume.

The crown should have enough structural projection to remain clearly distinct from the undercut without becoming inflated or excessively voluminous.

The undercut should remain close to the head.

The contrast should come from length and structure rather than exaggerated volume.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIR PHYSICS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair should behave as individual fibres and natural strand groups.

Preserve gravity, compression, overlap, and localized settling.

Allow subtle independent behaviour between neighbouring crown sections.

The clipped region should display authentic short-hair grain and microscopic directional variation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ROOT & HAIRLINE INTEGRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crown should emerge naturally from the scalp with realistic density and believable root transitions.

The clipped undercut should transition naturally into the existing hairline and scalp.

Preserve subtle irregularity around the temples and ears rather than creating mechanically perfect edges.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE RECOGNITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Within one second of viewing, the hairstyle should be unmistakably recognizable as a modern Undercut Pixie.

The viewer should immediately perceive:

• long textured crown
• extremely short clipped undercut
• strong crown-to-side contrast
• continuous undercut plane
• natural crown overhang
• controlled piece-y texture
• authentic salon craftsmanship

The undercut must remain the dominant architectural feature.

If the haircut can reasonably be interpreted as an ordinary pixie, increase the visual contrast between the long crown and clipped lower region while maintaining natural hair behaviour.

The final hairstyle must communicate a professionally executed Undercut Pixie rather than a classic pixie, tapered pixie, bixie, fade, crop, or short bob.
