# Blunt Lob — QA Specification

At normal viewing distance the result must immediately read as:

TRUE BLUNT LOB

with:

• Lob length
• continuous blunt perimeter
• dense lower edge
• minimal layering

PASS:

• collarbone to upper-shoulder termination
• front approximately aligned with sides
• minimal natural length variation
• no exaggerated front extension

FAIL:

• Bob
• French Bob
• Italian Bob
• Soft Lob
• grown-out long hair
• Butterfly Cut

PASS:

• one continuous cutting line
• strong visual density
• clean lower edge
• minimal microscopic variation

FAIL:

• feathering
• tapering
• wispy ends
• transparent ends
• broken perimeter
• visible layering

PASS:

• substantial lower-third weight
• dense healthy ends
• balanced left/right density

FAIL:

• airy ends
• excessive thinning
• graduated lower mass
• visible weight removal

• front sections integrate with perimeter
• no dramatic face framing
• no curtain sections
• no elongated front pieces

• dramatic movement
