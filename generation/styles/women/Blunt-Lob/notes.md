STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Blunt Lob is defined primarily by:

1. True Lob length
2. One continuous blunt perimeter
3. Substantial lower-third weight
4. Minimal visible layering
5. Restrained movement

The haircut should look deliberately cut rather than naturally grown.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The perimeter communicates the haircut.

The viewer should recognize:

BLUNT LOB

before noticing:

• texture
• shine
• movement
• individual fibres

PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. SOFT LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• perimeter becomes soft
• ends become wispy
• excessive movement
• lower weight disappears

Correction:

Strengthen the continuous perimeter.
Restore dense ends.

2. ITALIAN BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• too short
• excessive volume
• rounded shape
• blowout behaviour

Correction:

Restore Lob length.
Reduce volume.
Straighten the silhouette.

3. FRENCH BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• too short
• light perimeter
• breathable structure

Correction:

Restore collarbone/upper-shoulder length.
Increase lower weight.

4. BUTTERFLY DRIFT

Symptoms:

• visible layers
• face-framing sections
• cascading movement

Correction:

Remove visible layering.
Reconnect front sections to perimeter.

5. LONG-LAYER DRIFT

Symptoms:

• internal layers
• preserved long ends
• inconsistent weight

Correction:

Restore one continuous perimeter.

6. GROWN-OUT HAIR DRIFT

Symptoms:

• no obvious cutting line
• uneven termination
• weak lower density

Correction:

Strengthen the blunt perimeter.

7. HELMET DRIFT

Symptoms:

• synthetic continuous surface
• no fibre-level variation

Correction:

Introduce microscopic fibre variation while preserving
architectural control.

PERIMETER RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The lower edge must remain visually continuous.

The final 5–8 mm remain weighted.

Do not dissolve the perimeter through:

• tapering
• feathering
• strand breakup
• transparency

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MOVEMENT RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Movement is subordinate to the perimeter.

Only extreme outer fibres may move independently.

The main hair mass remains stable.
