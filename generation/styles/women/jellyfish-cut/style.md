# Jellyfish Cut

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY

Modify only the hair. The face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact head position, tilt and viewing angle. Any natural head tilt in the source is intentional and must remain unchanged. Jaw shape and width are locked. Chin is locked. Neck is locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. Match source scale and framing exactly; no zoom, crop or reframe.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION — CRITICAL

Preserve exact facial and visible skin texture, pores, tone and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beauty filter, retouching, relighting or skin-tone drift. Hair must not cast artificial color onto skin. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY

STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, sampling hairline, temples, part and nape where visible.

STEP 2 — Ignore and discard color from background, lighting, environment, clothing and reflections.

STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact source root color and only natural variation already present. Do not invent highlights, lowlights or dye.

STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: would the reproduced hair color still look correct on a pure white background? If no, correct the contamination.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Create a true Jellyfish Cut through a deliberate two-tier architecture: a short rounded upper cap around the head and substantially longer straight lower lengths beneath it.

### TWO-TIER GEOMETRY
- Build a compact upper section ending around the jaw-to-neck region, forming a clear rounded cap.
- Preserve a substantially longer lower section that drops below the upper tier as a distinct length system.
- The upper tier must have a clean, readable perimeter rather than merely being face-framing layers.
- The lower tier should remain comparatively straight, long and continuous, with enough density to read as a separate lower mass.
- The transition between tiers must be intentional and immediately legible, but naturally integrated rather than appearing digitally pasted on.

### UPPER TIER
Keep the upper cap rounded and compact around the skull with controlled volume. Its perimeter should behave like a short bob-like shell.

### LOWER TIER
Maintain long, relatively straight lengths beneath the cap. Avoid excessive internal layering that would erase the two-tier silhouette.

### FINISH
Use natural straight-to-softly-textured strand grouping and realistic overlap at the tier boundary. Preserve believable root density and gravity.

### DIFFERENTIATION
The defining signal is short rounded upper cap + clearly longer lower hanging tier. It must not collapse into Hime Cut, Wolf Cut, Shag, Mullet, Long Layers or a standard bob with long face framing.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No Hime cut, wolf cut, shag, mullet, butterfly cut, long layers, ordinary bob, lob, layered bob, curtain bangs, soft face-framing-only haircut, single-tier haircut, disconnected floating panels, excessive crown volume, hard digital seam, wig effect, face changes, ear regeneration, skin changes, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The finished image must look like the same original photograph after a professional haircut, not a regenerated person or synthetic wig.
