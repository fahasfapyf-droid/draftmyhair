# Boyish Pixie

Create a true Boyish Pixie defined by compact low-volume proportions, short controlled sides and nape, and a slightly longer but restrained top/front.

## GEOMETRY
- Keep the sides and nape distinctly shorter than the top and front, but do not create an undercut, fade, or disconnected step.
- Retain enough top/front length to establish a genuine pixie silhouette rather than a uniform clipper crop.
- Keep the crown close to the skull with low, controlled projection; the hairstyle must not become rounded, lifted, or voluminous.
- Maintain a compact head-hugging silhouette with practical short-hair proportions.
- The top-to-side length relationship must come from the haircut itself, not from sweeping hair over a shorter region.

## WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
- Keep the sides and nape visually light and controlled.
- Retain moderate, even weight through the top/front without creating a heavy fringe or exaggerated crown.
- Avoid a dramatic disconnection between top and lower sections; the transition should be professionally integrated.
- Preserve a clean, restrained perimeter rather than a heavily textured or shaggy outline.

## TEXTURE AND SURFACE
- Use restrained natural texture with small coherent strand groups and subtle directional separation.
- Maintain matte-to-soft-satin photographic hair behaviour rather than glossy styling.
- Ends should show professional cutting with microscopic natural irregularity.
- Roots must emerge naturally with believable density and directional flow.
- Do not introduce a new fringe or side sweep unless it already exists in the source.

## STYLE RECOGNITION
The result must immediately read as a compact Boyish Pixie through its low-volume crown, short controlled sides and nape, slightly longer top/front, and neutral practical proportions.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No high-volume pixie, rounded feminine pixie, Bixie, bowl cut, crop, fade, undercut, mohawk, long bob, shaggy pixie, exaggerated crown, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
