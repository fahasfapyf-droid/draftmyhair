# Boyish Pixie

Create a true Boyish Pixie defined by a compact, low-volume silhouette, restrained texture, short controlled sides and nape, and deliberately neutral haircut proportions.

## HAIRCUT GEOMETRY
- Keep the sides and nape short and closely controlled without creating an undercut or fade.
- Retain slightly more length through the top and front than the sides and nape so the haircut remains a pixie rather than a uniform crop.
- Keep the crown low and close to the skull; do not create lifted or rounded volume.
- Maintain a compact, practical silhouette with clear short-hair structure.
- The haircut must read as intentionally boyish through proportion and low volume, not through styling or facial changes.

## WEIGHT AND TEXTURE
- Distribute weight evenly enough to avoid a dramatic disconnected effect.
- Use restrained, natural texture with small strand groups and subtle directional separation.
- Keep the surface natural rather than glossy or heavily styled.
- Ends should show controlled salon cutting with microscopic natural irregularity.
- Do not introduce dramatic fringe or exaggerated crown height unless already present.

## STYLE RECOGNITION
The result must immediately read as a compact Boyish Pixie: short controlled sides and nape, slightly longer low-volume top, restrained texture, and deliberately neutral proportions.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No feminine rounded pixie, high-volume pixie, bixie, bowl cut, crop, fade, undercut, mohawk, exaggerated crown, long bob, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
