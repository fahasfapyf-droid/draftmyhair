Old Money Bob is an architectural luxury bob.

Core architecture:

CHIN LENGTH
+
DEEP SIDE PART
+
HEAVY/LIGHT SIDE WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
+
STRONG INWARD C-CURVE
+
POLISHED SALON FINISH

PRIMARY RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut must look expensive because of:

• precision
• weight
• controlled geometry
• polished finish
• intentional side-part architecture

It must not depend on excessive styling or artificial shine.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. LOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• hair below chin
• hair approaching shoulders
• extended front sections

Correction:

Restore chin-level perimeter.

2. GENERIC BLUNT BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• deep side part disappears
• both sides become visually identical
• C-curve becomes weak

Correction:

Restore asymmetric side-part weight and strong inward C-curve.

3. ITALIAN BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• silhouette becomes too rounded
• overall mass becomes too uniform
• side-part dominance decreases

Correction:

Strengthen architectural side-part structure.

4. FRENCH BOB DRIFT

Symptoms:

• haircut becomes too short
• texture becomes softer
• polished blowout character disappears

Correction:

Restore chin-length structure and refined salon finish.

5. OVER-PERFECT DIGITAL DRIFT

Symptoms:

• ruler edge
• plastic shine
• completely uniform strands
• synthetic silhouette

Correction:

Introduce microscopic optical variation without weakening
architectural precision.

6. EARRING COVERAGE

Symptoms:

• earrings disappear
• hair artificially covers ear area

Correction:

Allow the light side to tuck naturally behind/away from the ear.

Never modify the ear.
