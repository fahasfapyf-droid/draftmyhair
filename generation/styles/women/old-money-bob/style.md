# Draft My Hair Style Engine — v1

Create a genuine modern Old Money Bob.

The haircut must immediately communicate:

• architectural precision
• chin-length structure
• deep side part
• controlled asymmetric weight distribution
• polished luxury finish
• strong inward C-curve
• premium salon craftsmanship

The style should feel:

• polished
• expensive
• controlled
• sophisticated
• quietly powerful
• professionally finished

It must NOT read as:

• Lob
• Soft Bob
• French Bob
• Italian Bob
• Casual Bob
• Wavy Bob
• Blunt Lob
• Generic Blunt Bob

• head position

• head rotation
• camera angle
• camera perspective

• jaw width
• chin position
• chin projection
• neck shape
• ear shape
• ear size
• ear position

• body posture
• scale
• framing
• crop
• zoom
• composition

Do not rotate the head.

Do not straighten the head.

Do not reposition the head.

If the source contains a natural tilt or three-quarter orientation,
preserve it exactly.

The source photograph is the geometric authority.

The hairstyle must adapt to the source geometry.

The photograph must never adapt to the hairstyle.

The skull is a rigid anatomical structure.

Only the surface hair changes.

HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Determine intrinsic hair colour exclusively from the original root
zone.

Read approximately the first 2–3 mm emerging from the scalp across
visible:

• frontal hairline
• temples
• part
• crown
• nape

Use multiple visible root points.

Ignore environmental colour from:

• background
• clothing
• skin
• reflected surroundings
• lighting

Reproduce the exact intrinsic source colour.

Preserve only natural tonal variation already present.

Do not:

• invent highlights
• change colour family
• warm the hair
• cool the hair
• increase saturation
• flatten multi-tonal colour
• introduce artificial gloss

The blonde, brown, black, red, grey, or other source colour must
remain the source colour.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LENGTH — NON-NEGOTIABLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hair terminates at chin level.

The intended perimeter sits precisely around the chin landmark.

No hair extends below chin level.

No section approaches shoulder level.

No section touches the shoulders.

No Lob length.

No collarbone length.

No extended front pieces.

The haircut must immediately read as a chin-length bob.

The chin-level relationship must remain consistent with the source
pose and camera perspective.

Do not alter the chin or jaw to make the haircut appear correctly
positioned.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a strong, continuous, precise lower perimeter.

The perimeter should appear highly controlled and professionally cut.

Maintain approximately equal termination height on both sides while
respecting the source perspective.

The line must appear precise rather than artificially pasted.

Allow only microscopic optical variation caused by:

• individual strands
• camera resolution
• natural hair behaviour

Do not introduce obvious asymmetry.

Do not create an accidental Lob.

Do not create uneven length.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
DEEP SIDE PART
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a clearly defined deep side part.

The part must establish the entire weight distribution of the haircut.

The heavier side carries greater visual mass.

The lighter side remains controlled and slightly less voluminous.

The part should feel deliberate and sophisticated.

It must not appear:

• centered
• accidental
• overly messy
• artificially carved

The side part is one of the primary identifiers of the style.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HEAVY SIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The heavier side sweeps forward from the root.

Maintain controlled volume from root through the mid-length.

The hair should frame the face intentionally.

The mass must remain polished and cohesive.

Avoid:

• loose casual strands
• excessive puffiness
• uncontrolled separation
• exaggerated volume

The heavier side creates the characteristic Old Money Bob weight.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LIGHT SIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The lighter side remains close and controlled.

It carries slightly less visual mass than the heavy side.

It must not become completely flat.

Maintain enough density to preserve balance.

Where earrings are present, allow the hair to tuck naturally behind
or away from the ear so the earring remains visible.

The lighter side must remain intentional rather than neglected.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHAPE — ARCHITECTURAL PRECISION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a controlled weighted oval when viewed from the front.

The silhouette should be:

• controlled at the crown
• full through the mid-section
• progressively directed inward toward chin level

No:

• bubble shape
• mushroom shape
• helmet shape
• triangular expansion
• excessive width
• excessive crown height

The architecture should come from:

• side-part direction
• controlled density
• chin-length perimeter
• inward C-curve

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain smooth controlled crown height.

Crown volume comes primarily from the direction of the side sweep.

Do not create volume by inflating the crown.

Avoid:

• puffiness
• excessive lift
• rounded crown bubble
• inflated silhouette

The crown remains polished and controlled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
C-CURVE — DEFINING FEATURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a strong inward C-curve at the ends.

Both sides should curve naturally toward the face around chin level.

The C-curve should be:

• deliberate
• elegant
• controlled
• continuous
• physically believable

The curve must not resemble curling-iron curls.

It should appear to be created by:

• professional round-brush technique
• controlled blowout
• natural weight
• haircut architecture

Avoid:

• exaggerated curl
• ringlet behaviour
• repeated artificial curves
• wave patterns

The inward bend is a defining feature of the Old Money Bob.

• smooth strand alignment

• shag texture
• chunky separation
• frizz

Specular highlights should:

Avoid plastic-looking hair.

Avoid completely uniform shine.

STYLE DISTINCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OLD MONEY BOB:

→ chin length
→ deep side part
→ controlled asymmetric weight
→ strong inward C-curve
→ polished salon finish
→ architectural precision

ITALIAN BOB:

→ denser rounded silhouette
→ more relaxed luxury
→ stronger overall weight
→ less architectural side-part dominance

FRENCH BOB:

→ shorter
→ softer
→ often more compact
→ less controlled luxury blowout

BLUNT BOB:

→ stronger uniform blunt perimeter
→ less side-part-driven architecture
→ less pronounced C-curve

LOB:

→ longer than chin
→ extends toward shoulders/collarbone

This style must remain clearly distinct from all five.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SELF-CORRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If the result reads as Lob:

→ shorten the visible lower perimeter to chin level.

If the result reads as Italian Bob:

→ strengthen the deep side part and architectural control.

If the result reads as French Bob:

→ increase polished length toward chin level and strengthen the
   luxury blowout.

If the result reads as generic Blunt Bob:

→ strengthen side-part weight distribution and inward C-curve.

If the result becomes too geometric:

→ retain microscopic natural strand variation without weakening
   the overall architectural structure.

If the crown becomes too large:

→ reduce crown height and return volume to the side-sweep direction.

If earrings become covered:

→ restore natural ear clearance without modifying the ear itself.
