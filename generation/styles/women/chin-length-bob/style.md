# Chin-Length Bob

Create a true chin-length bob whose defining measurement is a continuous perimeter terminating at the chin region, with no drift into jaw-length micro proportions or lob length.

## LENGTH AND PERIMETER
- Set the primary lower perimeter at the chin region, using the subject's actual chin position as the anatomical length reference.
- The side and back perimeter must remain coherent with this chin-level target rather than producing isolated shorter or longer sections.
- Keep the silhouette compact and proportionate to the fixed head geometry.
- The cut must remain visibly longer than a micro/jaw-length bob but clearly shorter than a lob.
- Preserve a continuous, readable bob outline around the lower head.

## INTERNAL STRUCTURE
- Keep internal layering restrained so chin-level length remains the dominant structural signal.
- Preserve moderate lower weight beneath the surface.
- Avoid heavy graduation, feathering, shagging, or aggressive texturizing that would break the bob perimeter.
- Face-framing pieces remain subordinate to the chin-length architecture unless already present.

## WEIGHT AND SURFACE
- Maintain controlled natural volume that follows the fixed skull.
- Allow the hair to fall naturally toward the chin-level perimeter under gravity.
- Use believable strand grouping, realistic root density, and subtle fibre variation.
- The cut edge should be professionally controlled but retain microscopic natural irregularity; never create a ruler-perfect CGI line.
- Do not introduce bangs or new face framing unless they already exist.

## STYLE DIFFERENTIATION
The defining combination is precise chin-region termination + compact continuous bob structure + moderate lower weight. Do not let styling, curl, volume, or camera perspective obscure the intended chin-level length.

## STYLE RECOGNITION
The result must immediately read as a premium chin-length bob, with the chin serving as the clear anatomical endpoint of the primary perimeter.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No micro bob, jaw-length bob, blunt lob, long bob, shaggy bob, heavily layered bob, excessive graduation, wispy disconnected perimeter, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
