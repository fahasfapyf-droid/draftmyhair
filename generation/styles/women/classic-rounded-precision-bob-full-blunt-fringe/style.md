# Classic Rounded Precision Bob + Full Blunt Fringe

Create a true rounded precision bob with one unmistakable secondary feature: a full, dense, blunt fringe. The bob and fringe must read as one professionally engineered haircut.

## BOB GEOMETRY

- Build a compact rounded bob with a clean, continuous lower perimeter around the jaw-to-upper-neck region.
- Keep the silhouette softly rounded and controlled, with concentrated lower weight and minimal visible internal layering.
- Maintain balanced left/right geometry and avoid pronounced A-line, asymmetry, shag structure, or airy layering.
- The rounded shape must come from the haircut's internal weight and perimeter, not from exaggerated curling or blow-drying.

## FULL BLUNT FRINGE

- Create a substantial full fringe spanning the natural frontal hairline with a clean, predominantly horizontal lower edge.
- Keep fringe density continuous and substantial from temple-side transition to temple-side transition without turning into curtain bangs or separated wisps.
- Set the fringe at a natural brow-to-upper-forehead level appropriate to the fixed source anatomy; do not alter the forehead, brows, eyes, or facial proportions.
- Integrate the fringe into the crown and side sections without a visible attachment seam.

## FINISH AND DIFFERENTIATION

Use restrained natural surface variation, realistic root emergence, and microscopic edge irregularity. The defining combination is rounded precision bob + unmistakably full blunt fringe.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No curtain bangs, wispy fringe, side fringe, French Bob, Italian Bob, shaggy bob, lob, asymmetrical bob, heavy face-framing layers, or artificial wig effect. Do not alter the face, jaw, chin, neck, ears, skin, lighting, background, framing, perspective, or source hair colour.
