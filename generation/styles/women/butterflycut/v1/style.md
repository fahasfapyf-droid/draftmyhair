# Butterfly Cut

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE INFORMATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Style Name: Butterfly Cut

Category: Women's Haircut

Length: Long

Difficulty: High

Primary Characteristic:

A premium salon-quality layered haircut that visually resembles two complete haircuts layered together—a dominant shoulder-length haircut sitting above a preserved long foundation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create an authentic modern Butterfly Cut while preserving the subject's original long hair length.

The finished hairstyle must immediately and unmistakably read as a true Butterfly Cut rather than generic long layered hair.

The haircut should appear professionally designed, balanced, naturally wearable, and suitable for a luxury salon portfolio.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIRCUT DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The hairstyle must visually resemble two complete haircuts layered together.

The upper Butterfly Cut must visually dominate the hairstyle.

The preserved long foundation exists only to maintain overall length.

The viewer should immediately perceive:

• a complete shoulder-length haircut
• preserved long hair beneath

The hairstyle must never read as one continuous curtain of long layered hair.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The hairstyle must display two distinct silhouettes.

Upper silhouette:

• complete shoulder-length haircut
• visually dominant
• independently recognizable

Lower silhouette:

• preserved long foundation
• naturally emerging beneath the upper haircut
• secondary in visual importance

Both silhouettes must remain readable even when the hair is perfectly still.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERALL LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the original long foundation extending well below the shoulders.

Maintain overall length by removing internal weight rather than shortening the perimeter.

The preserved length should support the hairstyle without becoming its dominant visual feature.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UPPER BUTTERFLY STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The shoulder-length Butterfly structure is the primary haircut.

It must possess its own:

• silhouette
• perimeter
• density
• movement
• weight

The shoulder-length perimeter should remain continuously readable across the front of the hairstyle.

Its visible silhouette should terminate approximately between the collarbone and upper chest depending on the original hair length.

The upper structure should project slightly outward from the long foundation, creating subtle depth and natural overlap.

A viewer should be able to mentally remove the lower foundation and still perceive a complete shoulder-length haircut.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ARCHITECTURAL PRIORITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Butterfly structure is visually dominant.

The preserved long foundation is visually secondary.

At first glance the eye should perceive:

1. Shoulder-length Butterfly Cut
2. Preserved long foundation beneath

The upper structure should naturally conceal portions of the lower foundation across the front and sides.

If the hairstyle reads primarily as generic long layered hair, increase the visual dominance of the Butterfly structure without reducing overall length.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LOWER FOUNDATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The preserved long foundation should emerge naturally beneath the Butterfly structure.

Its purpose is to preserve length while supporting the upper haircut.

The lower foundation should appear lighter than traditional long hair because substantial internal weight has been removed.

It must never overpower the upper Butterfly silhouette.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FACE FRAMING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create elegant face-framing layers beginning between the cheekbone and chin.

The framing should flow directly into the upper Butterfly structure.

Both sides should remain balanced while respecting the natural hair parting.

Avoid disconnected framing sections.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LAYER FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create clearly visible cascading layers extending from the face toward the back.

Each successive layer should appear slightly longer than the previous layer.

Layer groups should remain visually identifiable while blending smoothly.

Avoid broad uninterrupted panels of equal-length hair.

Natural air gaps should separate neighboring layer groups while preserving continuity.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INTERNAL WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Remove weight primarily from the interior.

Maintain natural perimeter density.

Create subtle air pockets between neighboring layer groups.

The hairstyle should appear lightweight, flexible, and dynamic without appearing thin.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MOVEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Movement should originate from the haircut architecture rather than styling.

The upper Butterfly structure should gently lift away from the head before naturally settling over the preserved foundation.

Avoid exaggerated feathering or dramatic flipping.

The haircut must remain recognizable without wind or motion.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERIMETER & ENDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Finish every layer with refined salon point-cutting.

Ends should display subtle natural variation.

Maintain a soft premium finish.

Avoid:

• blunt edges
• shredded texture
• harsh perimeter lines

The shoulder-length perimeter should terminate in multiple visually independent feathered sections.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL HIERARCHY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Within the first second of viewing the observer should naturally perceive:

1. Elegant face-framing layers.
2. A complete shoulder-length Butterfly Cut.
3. The preserved long foundation beneath.

This hierarchy is mandatory.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE VALIDATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before considering the hairstyle complete, confirm:

✓ The upper Butterfly structure remains recognizable even if the lower foundation were visually hidden.

✓ The haircut cannot reasonably be mistaken for generic long layers.

✓ The shoulder-length Butterfly structure remains visually dominant.

✓ The preserved long foundation supports rather than hides the Butterfly Cut.

If any condition is not satisfied, refine only the haircut architecture until all conditions are met.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RECOGNITION TEST

The finished hairstyle must be unmistakably identifiable as a modern Butterfly Cut at normal conversational viewing distance.

The hairstyle should communicate:

• a clearly defined shoulder-length Butterfly structure
• preserved long foundation
• elegant face framing
• visible cascading layer groups
• lightweight internal architecture
• premium salon finish

The hairstyle must immediately read as a professionally executed Butterfly Cut rather than long hair with subtle layers.
