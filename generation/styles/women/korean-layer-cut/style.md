# Korean Layer Cut

Create a true modern Korean Layer Cut defined by a long polished foundation, deliberate front-to-mid internal layering, soft diagonal face framing and a controlled low-volume crown.

## FOUNDATION
- Maintain a medium-to-long overall length; do not shorten into bob or lob territory.
- Keep the external silhouette elongated and clean rather than shaggy or wide.
- The longest lower foundation remains continuous and visibly dominant.

## LAYER ARCHITECTURE
- Establish the first clearly visible framing layer around cheekbone-to-jaw level.
- Add a second longer layer through the upper-to-mid side sections.
- Continue the layers backward and downward so each transition merges into the main length rather than forming a panel.
- Concentrate the strongest visible layering around the front and mid-lengths; the lower foundation stays comparatively stable.
- Keep the crown smooth and low-volume, with no short stacked cap.

## FACE FRAMING
Use soft diagonal sections that follow the cheek and jaw before flowing backward into longer layers. The framing should visibly shape the face area without becoming curtain bangs, a blunt fringe or disconnected face panels.

## TEXTURE / FINISH
Use smooth salon-polished strand grouping with controlled inward/outward movement at selected ends. Layer transitions remain visible enough to identify the haircut but low-contrast and continuous. Preserve natural texture rather than forcing perfectly straight hair.

## STYLE DIFFERENTIATION
Korean Layer Cut = long polished foundation + multiple integrated front/mid layers + soft diagonal framing + controlled crown.
Distinct from Hush by clearer front/mid layer hierarchy and more polished directional movement; from Butterfly by less dramatic wing volume; from Shag/Wolf by smoother, less fragmented structure; from Curtain Layers by the framing remaining part of a layered haircut rather than two curtain panels.

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No hush cut, butterfly cut, wolf cut, shag, mullet, curtain bangs, curtain-layer panels, disconnected face framing, blunt bob, lob, bixie, pixie, high crown volume, choppy texture, exaggerated wings or generic one-layer long haircut.
