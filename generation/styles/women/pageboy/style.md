# Pageboy

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hair-adjacent area required to reveal anatomy naturally hidden by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, white balance, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. The hairstyle must conform to the source skull rather than changing head geometry. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, airbrushing, relighting, brightening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone. Hair must not cast artificial color onto the face or exposed neck.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true intrinsic hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact root color with only natural variation already present in the source.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine Pageboy: a compact rounded haircut with a continuous curved perimeter, controlled inward-turning ends and an integrated fringe. The haircut should look deliberately shaped around the head rather than like a generic bob with bangs.

### OVERALL SILHOUETTE
- Build a compact, smooth, rounded silhouette that follows the skull and upper jaw/upper-neck region.
- The haircut is shorter and more compact than a lob and should not extend toward the shoulders.
- Maintain controlled fullness through the sides and back without creating a helmet or mushroom effect.
- The shape should feel continuous from fringe through side perimeter into the nape.

### PERIMETER / LENGTH
- Establish a coherent perimeter around the jaw-to-upper-neck region, with the back slightly shorter or tucked under naturally.
- The visible lower edge should curve continuously rather than forming a straight blunt bob line.
- Keep the perimeter close enough to the head to remain recognizably Pageboy.
- Ends turn subtly inward toward the face and neck; the bend is a haircut/styling consequence, not a curl pattern.

### FRINGE
- Create a full, integrated fringe across the forehead when the source framing allows it.
- The fringe is substantial and controlled, not wispy curtain bangs.
- It connects directly into the side perimeter and continues the rounded architecture.
- Keep the fringe length around brow to upper-forehead level, adapting naturally to source perspective without altering the forehead or facial geometry.

### SIDES / NAPE
- Sides remain smooth, rounded and close to the head.
- The lower side sections curve inward toward the jaw/upper-neck perimeter.
- Nape hair follows the neck naturally and tucks inward rather than forming a bob-like outward shelf.
- Do not expose or reshape the ears merely to force the haircut geometry.

### TEXTURE / FINISH
Preserve the source hair texture while maintaining a smooth, controlled surface. Use fine strand grouping, realistic density, subtle edge irregularity and physically believable inward movement. Avoid excessive texture, curls, beach waves or editorial styling.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Pageboy = compact rounded silhouette + integrated full fringe + continuous curved perimeter + inward-turning ends + controlled nape.
Distinct from Bowl Cut, which has a higher bowl contour and more substantial upper cap; from Bob, which has a more explicit lower perimeter and less integrated fringe; and from French Bob, which is shorter, softer and less uniformly rounded.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No bowl cut, mushroom cut, generic bob, blunt bob, French bob, Italian bob, lob, pixie, crop, curtain bangs, wispy fringe, side-swept fringe, long face-framing pieces, shoulder length, collarbone length, outward-flipped ends, ringlets, beach waves, inflated crown, helmet volume, artificial scalp exposure, wig effect, pasted edges, facial changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally cut authentic Pageboy, with the continuous rounded perimeter and integrated fringe immediately recognizable.
