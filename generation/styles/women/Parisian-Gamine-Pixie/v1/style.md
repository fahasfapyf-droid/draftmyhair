# PARISIAN GAMINE PIXIE
# Draft My Hair Style Engine — v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE TARGET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a genuine modern Parisian Gamine Pixie.

The hairstyle should feel:

• short
• airy
• effortless
• feminine
• understated
• lightly structured
• naturally lived-in
• Parisian rather than editorial

The haircut should feel cut rather than styled.

Its architecture must remain softer and lighter than the Mia Farrow 1968 Pixie.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE IDENTITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The defining characteristics are:

1. Wispy micro-fringe
2. Airy crown
3. Fine strand separation
4. Lightweight compact silhouette
5. Softly tapered sides
6. Softly tapered nape
7. Subtle natural irregularity
8. Parisian minimalism

The haircut should remain clearly pixie-length.

It must never become a bob, bixie, crop, or heavily styled editorial pixie.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Crown:

Approximately 2.5–3 inches.

The crown should be slightly longer and lighter than the Mia Farrow 1968 Pixie.

Sides:

Softly tapered.

Ears:

Partially visible.

Nape:

Softly tapered.

Nothing should approach jaw length.

The overall silhouette must remain clearly short.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SILHOUETTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a rounded but lightweight silhouette.

The shape should feel lighter and less compact than an ultra-short precision pixie.

Hair should naturally follow the skull.

Do not create geometric compression.

Do not create excessive width.

Do not create heavy stacking.

Do not create a rigid Sassoon silhouette.

The haircut should feel naturally formed rather than architecturally imposed.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRINGE — DEFINING FEATURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a wispy micro-fringe.

The fringe should graze the eyebrows or sit slightly above them.

Individual strand lengths should vary naturally.

Maintain visible small air gaps between strand groups.

The fringe should appear semi-transparent against the forehead.

Allow subtle natural shadow overlap.

The fringe must never form a solid horizontal edge.

The fringe should feel:

• unplanned
• controlled
• delicate
• natural
• lightly irregular

Do not create:

• blunt fringe
• curtain bangs
• side sweep
• heavy fringe
• dense forehead coverage
• deliberate diagonal styling

The micro-fringe is the primary visual signature of this hairstyle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRINGE DENSITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Keep fringe density light.

Allow visible separation between fine strand groups.

The forehead should remain partially visible through the fringe.

Avoid creating a dense opaque curtain.

Do not blur or obscure the forehead.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CROWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create subtle lift without creating obvious volume.

Allow controlled air separation between fine strand groups.

Maintain:

• irregular strand breakup
• non-uniform density
• subtle natural layering
• varied strand direction

Avoid:

• sculpted styling
• symmetrical strand patterns
• uniform chunk grouping
• inflated crown
• modern fluffy volume

Allow approximately 2–4 extremely fine natural flyaways around the crown only.

Flyaways must remain subtle and physically believable.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SIDES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Sides should be softly tapered.

They should remain close to the skull without becoming ultra-flat.

Allow natural variation in length.

Avoid:

• bulky side panels
• excessive width
• hard clipper geometry
• aggressive undercutting
• overly exposed ears

The ears should remain partially visible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
NAPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Keep the nape softly tapered.

The nape should remain short without appearing shaved.

Avoid:

• long nape pieces
• bob-like neck coverage
• harsh undercut geometry
• heavy density at the neckline

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create fine natural strand separation.

Allow subtle variation in:

• strand width
• strand direction
• density
• length
• overlap

The texture should feel natural rather than deliberately constructed.

Maintain a matte finish.

Avoid:

• sleek polished surfaces
• spiky texture
• romantic waves
• messy editorial breakup
• chunky separation
• repeated strand patterns

Hair should feel cut, not styled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FLOW

Hair follows natural growth direction.

Allow slight directional inconsistencies.

Do not mirror both sides.

Do not create repeated curvature.

Do not impose deliberate styling direction.

Movement should remain subtle and gravity-driven.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Allow subtle crown lift.

Do not create actual volume expansion.

The hair should remain close enough to the skull to preserve the pixie silhouette.

Avoid:

• inflated crown
• fluffy silhouette
• wide temples
• excessive fullness
• modern blowout volume

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain natural non-uniform density.

The crown may contain subtle air separation.

The fringe remains lightweight.

The sides remain softly tapered.

Do not create artificial thinning.

Do not create random transparent patches.

• natural root emergence

The scalp-to-hair boundary must remain natural.

Before completing the generation, evaluate the haircut as an experienced stylist.

The result should immediately read:

PARISIAN GAMINE PIXIE

If it becomes too much like Mia Farrow:

→ increase subtle crown air
→ soften the silhouette
→ allow slightly more length
→ reduce geometric precision

If it becomes a modern fluffy pixie:

→ reduce crown volume
→ reduce strand separation
→ tighten the silhouette

If it becomes a textured crop:

→ soften texture
→ reduce editorial breakup
→ restore rounded pixie structure

If it becomes a bixie:

→ shorten the lower sides
→ reduce perimeter weight
→ maintain pixie proportions
