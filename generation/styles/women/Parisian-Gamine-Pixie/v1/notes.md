# Parisian Gamine Pixie — Production Notes

Status: v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A short, airy, effortless Parisian pixie.

The haircut is structured but not geometric.

Its defining feature is the wispy micro-fringe.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY CHARACTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The style should feel:

• Parisian
• minimal
• feminine
• understated
• lightly irregular
• naturally worn

It should look cut rather than styled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Crown:

2.5–3 inches.

Sides:

Softly tapered.

Ears:

Partially visible.

Nape:

Softly tapered.

Fringe:

Wispy micro-fringe.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DIFFERENCE FROM MIA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Mia Farrow 1968 Pixie:

• shorter
• flatter
• more compact
• more architectural
• more precise

Parisian Gamine Pixie:

• slightly longer
• lighter
• airier
• softer
• more irregular
• micro-fringe driven

Do not merge the two styles.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DIFFERENCE FROM TEXTURED CROP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Parisian Gamine Pixie is softer and less editorial.

Texture must remain restrained.

The haircut should not become aggressively piece-y.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Mia drift

The haircut becomes too flat and precise.

Correction:

Restore subtle air separation and softness.

2. Modern fluffy pixie

The crown becomes too voluminous.

Correction:

Reduce crown lift and tighten the silhouette.

3. Textured crop drift

Texture becomes the dominant characteristic.

Correction:

Reduce strand breakup and restore rounded structure.

4. Bixie drift

Lower sections become too long or heavy.

Correction:

Shorten lower perimeter and restore pixie proportions.

5. Heavy fringe

Fringe becomes opaque.

Correction:

Reduce density and restore visible forehead gaps.
