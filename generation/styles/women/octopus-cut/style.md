# Octopus Cut

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hair-adjacent area required to reveal anatomy naturally hidden by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, white balance, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. The hairstyle must conform to the source skull rather than changing head geometry. No zoom, crop, reframe or perspective change.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture, expression and natural micro-variation. No smoothing, beautification, airbrushing, relighting, brightening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone. Hair must not cast artificial color onto the face or exposed neck.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true intrinsic hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce the exact root color with only natural variation already present in the source.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: the reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a genuine modern Octopus Cut. The defining architecture is a compact, rounded upper layer system that transitions into several visibly separated, elongated lower sections. It must look intentionally multi-sectioned and tentacle-like without becoming a Jellyfish Cut, Wolf Cut, Shag or Mullet.

### UPPER CAP / CROWN
- Build a shorter rounded upper structure from the crown through the upper sides.
- Keep the upper layer compact and visibly shorter than the longest lower lengths.
- The crown follows the skull closely with controlled lift; do not inflate the head.
- The upper structure must have enough weight to read as a haircut layer, not scattered face-framing pieces.

### SIDE / FACE-FRAMING STRUCTURE
- Create multiple integrated side sections descending from the upper structure toward cheekbone, jaw and upper-shoulder regions.
- These sections should be staggered rather than one continuous curtain panel.
- Keep left and right architecture broadly balanced while allowing natural optical variation.
- Face framing must connect into the lower sections rather than ending as isolated bangs.

### LOWER “TENTACLE” SECTIONS
- Establish several distinct elongated lower sections beneath the shorter upper layer.
- Each lower section should retain substantial length and terminate independently, producing visible vertical separation.
- Use approximately 4–8 major lower sections distributed across the visible sides and back; do not make them mechanically identical.
- The longest sections should remain clearly longer than the upper structure and should extend continuously downward according to the source framing.
- Gaps between major sections are created by real layer separation and shadow, not shaved channels or empty painted spaces.
- Sections remain connected at the roots and upper structure; this is layered hair, not detachable pieces.

### LENGTH HIERARCHY
Upper structure = clearly short.
Middle sections = intermediate.
Lower tentacle sections = substantially long.
The length hierarchy must be obvious from front and three-quarter views while preserving the source pose and framing. Do not shorten the lower foundation into a bob or lob.

### TEXTURE / MOVEMENT
Use controlled layered texture with natural strand grouping, fine separation and gravity-driven movement. The upper structure may have moderate piece definition; the long lower sections remain comparatively smooth and weighted. Ends are tapered and naturally irregular, not blunt or feathered into uniform wisps.

### VOLUME / WEIGHT
Keep the crown compact-to-moderate. Weight is concentrated in the long lower sections rather than a giant crown. The silhouette should be vertically elongated with controlled width. Avoid a shaggy halo, wolf-like crown explosion or mullet tail.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Octopus Cut = short rounded upper layer + multiple connected elongated lower sections + visible vertical separation + long weighted ends.
Distinct from Jellyfish Cut, which uses a clearer two-tier cap-and-lower-body architecture; Wolf Cut, which has more aggressive all-around internal fragmentation; Shag, which distributes layering throughout; and Mullet, which is defined by short front/sides versus a single dominant rear length.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No Jellyfish Cut, Wolf Cut, Shag, Modern Shag, Mullet, Modern Mullet, Butterfly Cut, generic long layers, blunt long haircut, bob, lob, single-sheet lower length, single giant tail, disconnected hair pieces, shaved channels, undercut, skin fade, excessive crown volume, mushroom silhouette, bowl silhouette, uniform curtain bangs, repetitive identical tentacles, artificial gaps, wig effect, pasted edges, facial changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed modern Octopus Cut, with the multi-section lower architecture immediately recognizable.
