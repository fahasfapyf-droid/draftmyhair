# Sleek-Back Pixie

## 1. INPAINT HAIR ONLY
Modify only the hair and the minimum hidden area required to reveal anatomy naturally covered by removed hair. Face, facial features, expression, eyes, brows, skin texture, skin tone, jawline, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, color balance, focus, grain, pose, framing, perspective and source scale remain completely untouched.

## 2. GEOMETRY LOCK — ABSOLUTE
Preserve exact head position, tilt, rotation and viewing angle. Jaw, chin and neck are locked. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the surface hair changes. No zoom, crop, reframe, perspective change or head repositioning.

## 3. SKIN PRESERVATION
Preserve exact skin tone, undertone, pores, texture and expression. No smoothing, beautification, relighting, brightening, darkening or color drift. Never lighten the source skin tone.

## 4. HAIR COLOR — ROOT AUTHORITY
STEP 1 — Read true hair color ONLY from the first 2–3 mm at visible scalp roots, hairline, temples, part, crown and nape.
STEP 2 — Ignore background, lighting, clothing and environmental reflections.
STEP 3 — Reproduce exact root color with only natural source variation.
STEP 4 — White-background contamination check: reproduced hair color must remain correct against pure white.

## 5. STYLE-SPECIFIC DESIGN
Create a refined Sleek-Back Pixie: true pixie proportions with the top/front directed backward in one controlled flow. Sleekness comes from directional organization and restrained surface tension, not wet product or added volume.

### PIXIE GEOMETRY
- Top/front longer than short tapered sides and nape.
- Compact skull-following silhouette; no lower section approaches bixie/bob/lob length.
- No undercut or shaved disconnect.

### BACKWARD FLOW
- Hair begins at the frontal hairline and travels backward through top and crown.
- Global rearward direction is unmistakable, while neighbouring fibres retain slight natural variation.
- Do not redirect laterally into a side fringe or vertically into a quiff.

### SURFACE / CROWN
- Hair stays close to the skull with controlled, non-plastic surface tension.
- Crown remains low-to-moderate and follows anatomy.
- Preserve visible fibre structure and fragmented natural highlights.

### HAIRLINE / EARS
Preserve original hairline irregularity, baby hairs and temple density. Hair sweeps naturally around existing ears; never carve openings or sharpen edges digitally.

### FINISH
Smooth, controlled, dry-to-natural and touchable. No wet, greasy, lacquered or mirror-gloss surface.

### DIFFERENTIATION
Sleek-Back Pixie = true pixie length + unified backward flow + low controlled volume + dry refined surface.
Distinct from Slick Back, Wet-Look Pixie, Quiff, Pompadour and Side-Fringe Pixie.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No bixie, bob, lob, undercut, shaved sides, wet look, greasy hair, lacquered shine, gelled editorial finish, quiff, pompadour, side fringe, curtain fringe, spiky pixie, fluffy pixie, crown bubble, artificial lift, plastic surface, painted hairline, carved ear openings, wig effect, face changes, skin changes, jaw reshaping, ear regeneration, head movement, lighting changes, background changes, zoom, crop or reframe.

The result must look like the same photograph after a professionally executed Sleek-Back Pixie.
