Sleek-Back Pixie is a true pixie whose identity comes from
controlled backward directional flow.

Core architecture:

TRUE PIXIE
+
BACKWARD FLOW
+
SURFACE TENSION
+
CLOSE SKULL FOLLOWING
+
NATURAL FIBRE STRUCTURE

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"Sleek" must describe directional organization.

It must NOT mean:

• wet
• greasy
• lacquered
• gelled
• plastic
• flattened

The result should appear naturally swept backward.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. WET-LOOK DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive gloss
• continuous reflective surface
• hair appears wet or oily

Correction:

Reduce gloss and surface compression.
Restore fragmented fibre highlights.

2. GELLED SLICK-BACK DRIFT

Symptoms:

• perfectly flattened hair
• rigid parallel strands
• heavy product appearance

Correction:

Introduce microscopic strand variation and natural tension.

3. FLUFFY PIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• crown lift
• excessive volume
• rounded silhouette

Correction:

Bring hair closer to skull.

4. SPIKY PIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• visible isolated spikes
• excessive piece separation
• aggressive texture

Correction:

Restore cohesive backward flow.

5. BIXIE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• excessive side length
• heavy nape
• bob-like lower perimeter

Correction:

Restore true pixie proportions.

6. SIDE-FRINGE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• movement becomes predominantly lateral
• visible diagonal fringe

Correction:

Restore unified backward movement.

7. HELMET DRIFT

Symptoms:

• continuous artificial shell
• no visible fibre structure
• perfectly uniform surface

Correction:

Restore microscopic fibre variation.
