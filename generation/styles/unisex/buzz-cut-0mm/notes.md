━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A true zero-guard clipper cut.

The scalp remains covered by extremely short visible hair fibres.

This is NOT a bald transformation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY DIFFERENCE FROM BALD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BALD:

Hair removed.

Scalp becomes the dominant visible surface.

BUZZ 0.0 MM:

Hair remains.

Follicular stubble creates visible short-hair density across the scalp.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The haircut follows the original hairline and skull geometry.

No new hairline is designed.

No skull shape is altered.

The visual result comes from:

• extremely short length
• follicular density
• natural growth direction
• scalp integration
• realistic lighting

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. BALD DRIFT

Symptoms:

• scalp appears completely hairless
• no follicular density

Correction:

Restore subtle visible follicular stubble.

2. GREY SCALP DRIFT

Symptoms:

• entire scalp becomes uniformly grey
• follicles become indistinguishable

Correction:

Restore individual irregular follicular density.

3. LONG BUZZ DRIFT

Symptoms:

• visible hair fibres become too long
• scalp begins to resemble a #1/#2 buzz

Correction:

Reduce apparent fibre length.

4. ARTIFICIAL STUBBLE

Symptoms:

• repeated dots
• uniform spacing
• procedural texture

Correction:

Restore irregular biological distribution.

5. HAIRLINE DRIFT

Symptoms:

• temples redesigned
• widow's peak changed
• frontal contour straightened

Correction:

Restore original hairline architecture.

FOLLICLE RULE

Follicles are physical hair structures, not decorative dots.

They should vary naturally in:

• density
• direction
• visibility
• local darkness
