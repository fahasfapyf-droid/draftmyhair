# CROWN THINNING

Create a genuine Crown Thinning. The defining architecture is localized vertex density reduction with stronger frontal and side coverage, irregular crown thinning around natural whorl.

Preserve the exact source face, expression, skin texture and tone, head position, skull geometry, jaw, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, framing, perspective, focus, grain and color balance. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the requested hair or scalp-hair density changes.

The result must look like a real professional transformation photographed in the original scene. Maintain realistic roots/follicles, density, shadow, strand variation and edge integration. No wig effect, pasted edge, halo, plastic texture or digital mask boundary.

STYLE DISTINCTION:
Crown Thinning must be immediately distinguishable from complete baldness, frontal recession, artificial circular patch, grey overlay.

NEGATIVE:
No complete baldness, frontal recession, artificial circular patch, grey overlay; no facial changes; no skin changes; no ear regeneration; no skull reshaping; no head-angle change; no lighting/background change; no crop/zoom/reframe; no synthetic texture.
