# CLEAN SHAVED HEAD

Create a genuine Clean Shaved Head. The defining architecture is complete razor-shaved scalp with source-consistent skin texture, subtle post-shave follicular perception, exact skull geometry.

Preserve the exact source face, expression, skin texture and tone, head position, skull geometry, jaw, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, framing, perspective, focus, grain and color balance. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the requested hair or scalp-hair density changes.

The result must look like a real professional transformation photographed in the original scene. Maintain realistic roots/follicles, density, shadow, strand variation and edge integration. No wig effect, pasted edge, halo, plastic texture or digital mask boundary.

STYLE DISTINCTION:
Clean Shaved Head must be immediately distinguishable from buzz cut, visible hair fibres, grey hair cap, plastic scalp.

NEGATIVE:
No buzz cut, visible hair fibres, grey hair cap, plastic scalp; no facial changes; no skin changes; no ear regeneration; no skull reshaping; no head-angle change; no lighting/background change; no crop/zoom/reframe; no synthetic texture.
