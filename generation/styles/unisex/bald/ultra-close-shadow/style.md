# ULTRA-CLOSE BALD SHADOW

Create a genuine Ultra-Close Bald Shadow. The defining architecture is ultra-close shaved scalp with no visible hair length but extremely subtle follicular shadow, visually between clean shave and buzz.

Preserve the exact source face, expression, skin texture and tone, head position, skull geometry, jaw, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, framing, perspective, focus, grain and color balance. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the requested hair or scalp-hair density changes.

The result must look like a real professional transformation photographed in the original scene. Maintain realistic roots/follicles, density, shadow, strand variation and edge integration. No wig effect, pasted edge, halo, plastic texture or digital mask boundary.

STYLE DISTINCTION:
Ultra-Close Bald Shadow must be immediately distinguishable from obvious stubble, 0 mm buzz, grey painted scalp, dot pattern.

NEGATIVE:
No obvious stubble, 0 mm buzz, grey painted scalp, dot pattern; no facial changes; no skin changes; no ear regeneration; no skull reshaping; no head-angle change; no lighting/background change; no crop/zoom/reframe; no synthetic texture.
