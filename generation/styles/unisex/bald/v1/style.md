# BALD / CLEAN SHAVED
# Draft My Hair Style Engine — v1

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE TARGET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a completely shaved bald-head appearance.

The result must look like the same person photographed naturally after shaving the hair completely.

The transformation is hair removal.

The scalp replaces the visible hair region.

The final result must communicate:

SAME PERSON
+
REALISTICALLY BALD

The result must never appear:

• edited
• painted
• digitally overlaid
• airbrushed
• plastic
• artificially smooth

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BALD STATE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Remove all visible scalp hair.

No visible hairstyle remains.

No visible hair mass.

No long hairs.

No isolated strands.

No sideburn remnants unless they naturally belong to an explicitly requested facial-hair region outside the hair mask.

The result should represent a fully shaved scalp.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCALP STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Reconstruct the scalp as natural human skin.

The scalp must follow the exact underlying skull geometry of the source.

Do not:

• round the skull
• enlarge the skull
• narrow the skull
• lengthen the skull
• flatten the skull
• reshape the head

The bald state should reveal the existing anatomical geometry rather than create new geometry.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCALP TONALITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Match the scalp to the person's existing skin characteristics.

Scalp tone must remain consistent with the source complexion.

Do not artificially lighten the scalp.

Do not artificially darken the scalp.

Do not introduce a generic pink, grey, beige, or pale scalp colour.

Preserve natural warmth and undertone.

The scalp should look like the same person's skin under the same photograph's lighting.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCALP MICRO-VARIATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The scalp must contain subtle natural variation.

Allow microscopic variation in:

• tone
• warmth
• texture
• density
• local skin response

Variation must remain organic and irregular.

Do not create symmetrical patterns.

Do not distribute variation uniformly.

Do not create visible artificial noise.

Do not create a flat colour overlay.

The scalp must retain believable human skin behaviour.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FOLLICLE PERCEPTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create extremely subtle follicle presence.

Follicles should be perceived primarily as tonal density rather than individually visible dots.

Maintain slightly greater perceived density around:

• crown
• temples

Allow softer density toward:

• frontal upper scalp

Allow slightly richer perceived density around:

• ear perimeter

Variation must remain subtle.

Do not render individually distinguishable follicle dots.

Do not create a stippled pattern.

Do not create noise-like follicle texture.

Do not make the scalp appear peppered with dots.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HAIRLINE TRANSITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The former hairline must transition naturally into the shaved scalp.

Do not create a hard artificial boundary.

Do not completely flatten the former hairline into an unnaturally uniform surface.

Allow a subtle tonal transition where hair previously emerged.

The transition should remain naturally imperfect.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The transition from forehead to scalp must appear anatomically continuous.

Preserve the natural forehead contour.

Maintain subtle tonal continuity.

Avoid:

• hard edges
• visible masking
• excessive blur
• artificial flattening
• abrupt colour changes

The scalp should appear to continue naturally from the forehead.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Match:

• lighting direction
• light intensity
• shadow direction
• shadow softness
• falloff
• exposure
• white balance

Light should naturally wrap around the existing skull curvature.

Maintain realistic gradients across:

• crown
• temples
• upper sides
• rear scalp where visible

Do not introduce a new lighting setup.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use natural tonal gradients to communicate skull curvature.

The scalp must not appear flat.

Do not exaggerate anatomical contours.

Do not sculpt artificial highlights.

Do not create dramatic cinematic shading.

The result should remain consistent with the source photograph.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Match:

• edge softness
• depth of field
• lens characteristics
• natural transition
• source grain

No cutout appearance.

No halo.

No hard digital edge.

No background spill beyond the reconstructed hair region.

The final result should immediately communicate:

FULLY SHAVED BALD HEAD

The viewer should not perceive:

• buzz cut
• stubble haircut
• hair cap
• grey overlay
• artificial scalp
• painted skin

If visible hair remains:

→ remove the remaining hair mass.

If the scalp appears too smooth:

→ restore subtle natural skin variation.

If the scalp appears too dotted:

→ reduce follicle visibility to tonal perception.

If the scalp appears lighter than the face:

→ restore source-consistent scalp tonality.

If the skull appears reshaped:

→ restore the original head geometry.
