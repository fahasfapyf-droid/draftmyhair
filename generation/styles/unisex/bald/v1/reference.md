REFERENCE PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Define the visual characteristics of a naturally shaved bald scalp.

The reference controls scalp appearance only.

The uploaded source photograph remains authoritative for:

• identity
• face
• pose
• camera
• lighting
• background
• framing
• skin complexion

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY REFERENCE CHARACTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A completely shaved human scalp with:

• natural skin texture
• subtle follicle density
• realistic scalp curvature
• natural tonal variation
• believable lighting response
• seamless hairline transition

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FOLLICLE INTERPRETATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Follicles should be perceived as subtle tonal density.

Do not interpret the reference as a requirement to render individual dots.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REFERENCE PROHIBITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Avoid references dominated by:

• visible stubble
• buzz cuts
• razor-sharp hairlines
• artificially smooth scalps
• plastic skin
• excessive follicle dots
• studio-generated bald-head renders
• beauty-retouched skin
• artificial scalp highlights
