# TAPERED BUZZ

Create a genuine Tapered Buzz. The defining architecture is short buzz with subtle progressive taper at sideburns and neckline, compact clipped top, no hard fade.

Preserve the exact source face, expression, skin texture and tone, head position, skull geometry, jaw, chin, neck, ears, body, clothing, background, lighting, exposure, framing, perspective, focus, grain and color balance. Ear shape locked — do not regenerate, reshape, enlarge, reduce, reposition, or reconstruct the ears. The skull is a rigid fixed object. Only the requested hair or scalp-hair density changes.

The result must look like a real professional transformation photographed in the original scene. Maintain realistic roots/follicles, density, shadow, strand variation and edge integration. No wig effect, pasted edge, halo, plastic texture or digital mask boundary.

STYLE DISTINCTION:
Tapered Buzz must be immediately distinguishable from skin fade, high-and-tight, long top, bald scalp.

NEGATIVE:
No skin fade, high-and-tight, long top, bald scalp; no facial changes; no skin changes; no ear regeneration; no skull reshaping; no head-angle change; no lighting/background change; no crop/zoom/reframe; no synthetic texture.


## SKIN PRESERVATION — ZERO TOLERANCE

Do not lighten, darken, smooth, blur, repaint or reconstruct facial or exposed scalp skin. Preserve source pores, texture, tone, highlights and shadows exactly. Do not introduce a generic pale, grey or relit skin surface.

## ROOT AUTHORITY

Read true source hair color and root architecture only from the first 2–3 mm at the scalp, hairline, temples, part and nape. Ignore background, lighting, clothing, reflections and global grading. Reproduce source root color, density, growth direction and natural variation. Do not invent root patterns.

## PHOTOREALISTIC INTEGRATION

Hair must emerge naturally from the scalp with realistic density, roots, strand hierarchy, growth direction, contact shadows, flyaways and edge behavior. Preserve original lighting, exposure, camera grain, noise, focus and color response. No wig effect, hard mask, halo, painted scalp, plastic texture or synthetic strand repetition.
