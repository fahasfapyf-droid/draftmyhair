# 1. Purpose

The Feedback System enables Draft My Hair to collect structured feedback from users after a hairstyle generation has been completed.

Its purpose is to measure customer satisfaction, identify quality issues, and provide engineering with reliable data for continuous product improvement.

The Feedback System is not designed to collect public reviews or testimonials. Instead, it serves as an internal product improvement mechanism that transforms real customer experiences into measurable engineering insights.

Every completed feedback submission contributes to improving hairstyle quality, identity preservation, prompt engineering, and the overall customer experience.

The Feedback System forms a core part of the Draft My Hair product lifecycle and supports evidence-based decision making across all hairstyle generation features.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 2. Vision

Draft My Hair aims to become the world's most trusted and photorealistic hairstyle preview platform.

Achieving this requires more than producing realistic images. It requires continuously learning from real customer experiences.

The Feedback System exists to bridge the gap between internal quality assurance and real-world usage.

Instead of relying solely on internal testing, Draft My Hair continuously measures how customers perceive the realism, accuracy, and usefulness of every hairstyle preview.

Over time, this information enables the platform to:

• Improve hairstyle realism.

• Improve identity preservation.

• Improve hairstyle specifications.

• Improve prompt engineering.

• Improve customer confidence.

• Improve product quality through measurable engineering decisions.

The long-term vision is that every completed generation contributes to making future generations more accurate, more realistic, and more useful.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 3. Objectives

The Feedback System shall support the following objectives.

Primary Objectives

• Measure overall customer satisfaction.

• Measure perceived identity preservation.

• Measure hairstyle decision confidence.

• Identify recurring quality issues.

• Collect actionable customer insights.

• Support evidence-based engineering decisions.

• Build historical product performance data.

Secondary Objectives

• Prioritize engineering improvements.

• Compare hairstyle performance.

• Compare prompt revisions.

• Compare specification revisions.

• Identify trends across hairstyle categories.

• Support long-term analytics and reporting.

The Feedback System exists to improve the product rather than evaluate individual users.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 4. Scope

Version 1.0 of the Feedback System includes:

• Overall Satisfaction Rating

• Identity Preservation Rating

• Real-Life Hairstyle Decision

• Improvement Categories

• Optional Written Comments

• Internal Analytics

• Historical Reporting

Version 1.0 does not include:

• Public reviews

• Public ratings

• Social interactions

• Screenshot uploads

• Customer support requests

• AI conversations

• Hairdresser reviews

• Email follow-up campaigns

• A/B testing

Features outside this scope require a future specification revision before implementation.

# 5. Product Principles

The Feedback System shall operate according to the following principles.

Optional Participation

Submitting feedback shall always remain optional.

Users shall retain full access to their generated hairstyle regardless of whether feedback is submitted.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Minimal Friction

The complete feedback process should require less than twenty seconds for the majority of users.

The system should minimize unnecessary interaction while collecting meaningful information.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Actionable Information

Every question presented to the user must support measurable product improvement.

Questions that do not contribute to engineering, analytics, or product quality shall not be included.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Structured Before Free Text

Whenever practical, structured responses shall be preferred over open-ended comments.

Structured data enables consistent reporting, trend analysis, and product improvement.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Privacy by Design

Only information required for product improvement shall be collected.

The system shall avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Continuous Improvement

Feedback collected through the system shall directly support future improvements to hairstyle specifications, prompt engineering, generation quality, and customer experience.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# 6. Feedback Lifecycle

The Feedback System shall be presented immediately after a hairstyle generation has been successfully completed.

Feedback is collected only after the customer has received access to their generated image.

Providing feedback shall never be a prerequisite for downloading or accessing a generated hairstyle.

The lifecycle of a feedback session is defined as follows.

Generation Completed

↓

Preview Displayed

↓

Image Available for Download

↓

Optional Feedback Invitation

↓

Customer Response

↓

Feedback Validation

↓

Feedback Storage

↓

Analytics Processing

↓

Engineering Review

↓

Future Product Improvements

Each feedback submission represents a completed evaluation of a single hairstyle generation.

A customer may choose to skip the feedback process without affecting access to the generated result.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 7. Feedback Questionnaire

Version 1.0 consists of five questions.

The questionnaire is intentionally designed to be short, structured, and easy to complete.

The expected completion time should remain below twenty seconds for most users.

Question 1

Overall Satisfaction

Purpose

Measure overall customer satisfaction with the generated hairstyle preview.

Response Type

Five-star rating.

Required

Yes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Question 2

Identity Preservation

Question

How much does this still look like you?

Purpose

Measure how successfully the hairstyle transformation preserves the customer's facial identity.

Response Type

Five-star rating.

Required

Yes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Question 3

Real-Life Decision

Question

Would you consider getting this hairstyle in real life?

Purpose

Measure whether the hairstyle preview increases customer confidence in making a haircut decision.

Response Type

Single choice.

Options

• Definitely Yes

• Probably Yes

• Not Sure

• Probably Not

• Definitely Not

Required

Yes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Question 4

Improvement Areas

Purpose

Identify the primary reason why the customer was not fully satisfied.

Display Rule

Displayed only when the Overall Satisfaction rating is less than five stars.

Response Type

Multiple choice.

Options

• Doesn't look like me

• Hairstyle doesn't match expectations

• Hair too long

• Hair too short

• Hair colour isn't accurate

• Hair texture looks unrealistic

• Hairline or scalp looks unnatural

• Lighting or background changed

• Other

Required

No.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Question 5

Additional Comments

Purpose

Allow customers to provide additional information that cannot be expressed through structured responses.

Response Type

Free text.

Maximum Length

500 characters.

Required

No.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 8. Submission Rules

The system shall validate every response before accepting a feedback submission.

Required questions must be completed before submission.

Optional questions may be left unanswered.

Each feedback submission shall represent one completed hairstyle generation.

Feedback shall not be editable after submission.

Multiple submissions for the same generation shall not be permitted.

Skipped feedback shall not be recorded as negative feedback.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 9. Success Criteria

The Feedback System shall be considered successful when it consistently produces reliable, actionable, and measurable product insights.

Key indicators include:

• High questionnaire completion rate.

• High response quality.

• Identification of recurring product issues.

• Clear engineering priorities.

• Reliable long-term trend analysis.

• Continuous improvement in customer satisfaction over time.

The success of the Feedback System is measured by the quality of the engineering insights it produces rather than the quantity of responses collected.

# 10. Engineering Data Requirements

Every feedback submission shall be permanently associated with the hairstyle generation that produced it.

The purpose of this association is to enable accurate engineering analysis, historical reporting, version comparisons, and future product improvements.

The Feedback System shall ensure that every feedback record can always be traced back to the exact generation that the customer evaluated.

Engineering data shall be stored automatically without requiring any additional input from the customer.

The customer shall only answer the questionnaire.

All engineering metadata shall be collected by the system.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Required Generation Information

Every feedback record shall be associated with the following generation information.

• Generation ID

• User ID (if authenticated)

• Anonymous Session ID (if unauthenticated)

• Generation Timestamp

• Hairstyle

• Source Image Identifier

• Generated Image Identifier

• Generation Status

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Required Engine Information

Every feedback record shall include the generation engine information used to create the hairstyle preview.

Examples include:

• Generation Engine Version

• Master Prompt Version

• Hairstyle Specification Version

• Prompt Builder Version

• AI Model

• AI Model Version

• Processing Time

• Generation Configuration

This information enables engineering teams to compare performance across different versions of the system.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Required Product Information

Every feedback submission shall also be associated with the product environment in which it was collected.

Examples include:

• Website Version

• Feedback Questionnaire Version

• Language

• Device Type

• Operating System

• Browser

• Screen Size Category

Collecting this information helps identify environment-specific issues and usability trends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Required Feedback Information

Each feedback record shall include:

• Submission Timestamp

• Overall Satisfaction Rating

• Identity Preservation Rating

• Real-Life Decision

• Improvement Categories

• Optional Written Comments

• Feedback Completion Status

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Data Integrity

Engineering metadata shall be generated automatically by the platform.

Customers shall not be permitted to modify engineering metadata.

Every feedback submission shall remain permanently associated with the generation that created it.

Historical records shall never be reassigned to different generations.

If future versions of the generation engine are released, previously submitted feedback shall continue to reference the exact versions that were originally used.

This ensures historical accuracy and enables meaningful comparison between product revisions.

# 11. Privacy Principles

The Feedback System shall collect only the information necessary to improve the Draft My Hair product.

Feedback data shall never be collected for advertising, profiling, or unrelated business purposes.

Participation in the Feedback System shall remain entirely voluntary.

Customers may choose to skip the questionnaire without affecting access to their generated hairstyle.

Personally identifiable information shall not be requested as part of the feedback questionnaire.

The system shall minimize data collection wherever possible while preserving the engineering insights required for product improvement.

Privacy considerations shall remain part of every future revision of the Feedback System.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 12. Future Evolution

Version 1.0 intentionally focuses on collecting structured product feedback with minimal user friction.

Future versions of the Feedback System may expand its capabilities as the platform grows.

Potential future enhancements include:

• Screenshot attachments

• Before and after comparison ratings

• Hairstylist reviews

• Follow-up satisfaction surveys

• Personalized hairstyle recommendations

• A/B testing of questionnaire variants

• Customer feature requests

• Multi-language questionnaires

• AI-assisted feedback summarization

Future enhancements shall be evaluated independently and require their own specification revisions before implementation.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 13. Success Metrics

The effectiveness of the Feedback System shall be evaluated using measurable product metrics rather than anecdotal observations.

Primary success indicators include:

• Feedback completion rate

• Average overall satisfaction rating

• Average identity preservation rating

• Percentage of users who would consider the hairstyle in real life

• Most frequently selected improvement areas

• Percentage of submissions containing written comments

• Satisfaction trends over time

• Satisfaction trends by hairstyle

• Satisfaction trends by engine version

• Satisfaction trends by specification version

The purpose of these metrics is to identify opportunities for continuous improvement rather than evaluate individual users.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 14. Out of Scope

The following features are explicitly excluded from Feedback System Version 1.0.

• Public reviews

• Public ratings

• Community discussions

• Customer support ticketing

• Social sharing

• Screenshot uploads

• Email campaigns

• Push notifications

• Loyalty programs

• Rewards for submitting feedback

• Public leaderboards

• Hairstylist verification

• AI-generated responses to feedback

Excluding these features keeps Version 1.0 focused, lightweight, and easy to complete while providing high-value engineering data.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# 15. Product Summary

The Feedback System is a core subsystem of Draft My Hair.

Its purpose is to transform customer experiences into structured engineering knowledge.

Every completed questionnaire contributes to improving hairstyle realism, identity preservation, prompt engineering, and overall product quality.

The Feedback System supports a continuous improvement cycle in which production usage directly informs future development.

By combining structured customer feedback with generation metadata, Draft My Hair can make evidence-based engineering decisions that improve future hairstyle generations while maintaining a fast, optional, and privacy-conscious experience for customers.
